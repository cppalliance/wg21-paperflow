# 22 - Grounding-Robustness-Auditor

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions   Grounding is active (9/197 sidecars dropped spans, `00-baseline.md:47`) and blocks obvious hallucinations, but whole-document `partial_ratio` at 0.90 plus OmniDocBench normalization accept short generic quotes and math-corruption quotes that never appeared verbatim, while `pass` verdicts require zero grounded evidence.
**Confidence:** high

## Findings
- [CRITICAL] Short generic quotes trivially exceed the 0.90 fuzzy floor against a whole-document haystack. Evidence: `grounding.py:51` applies `partial_ratio(norm_quote, norm_md) / 100.0 >= EVIDENCE_FUZZY_FLOOR` (`constants.py:50`) where `norm_md` is the entire paper (`grounding.py:39`); runtime on ~500k-char synthetic haystack: `"the following table"` → **1.0000**, `"the following"` → **1.0000**, `"the"` → **1.0000** (all pass); on WG21-like ~5k-word markdown: `"the following table"` → **0.9412**, `"the following"` → **0.9167**, `"summarizes the results"` → **1.0000** (reproduced 2026-07-03). Impact: an LLM can fabricate a plausible evidence quote from boilerplate WG21 phrasing and have it grounded without locating a contiguous supporting span.
- [CRITICAL] `normalized_text` erases the characters the math/tables axes police, so corrupted math quotes ground against faithful markdown. Evidence: `metrics.py:115-126` (`clean_string` keeps `\w` + CJK only, strips `^`, `_`, `|`, `$`); `grounding.py:39-44` normalizes both haystack and quote symmetrically. Runtime: markdown `"The energy is $E = mc^2$ as shown."` with fabricated quote `"$E = mc$"` → `norm_quote='Emc'`, substring **True** in `norm_md='TheenergyisEmc2asshown'`, `partial_ratio=1.0000`, `ground_spans` **grounded=1 dropped=0**; `"$x$"` and `"$\\sqrt{x}$"` both normalize to `'x'` and ground with `partial_ratio=1.0000` against md containing both forms. Impact: exponent loss, sqrt collapse, and fraction-wrapper drops that the `math` axis exists to catch can still produce grounded evidence spans.
- [HIGH] `pass` verdicts need no grounded evidence; demotion applies only to non-pass. Evidence: `adjudicate.py:237-238` (`if suggested_verdict != VERDICT_PASS and not grounded: suggested_verdict = VERDICT_REVIEW`); `tapetum_llm.md:99` instructs faithful conversions to return `pass` with empty evidence. Impact: the 73 production `pass` sidecars (`00-baseline.md:45`) carry zero machine-verified quotes; grounding cannot falsify a confident pass, only soften a fail/review that lacked support.
- [HIGH] Transpose/superscript corruption can pass fuzzy grounding at the 0.90 floor. Evidence: quote `"matrix A here"` against md `"We transpose matrix A^T here."` → `partial_ratio=0.9091` (reproduced 2026-07-03), `ground_spans` **grounded=1** (`grounding.py:51`, `EVIDENCE_FUZZY_FLOOR=0.90` at `constants.py:50`). Impact: `stable_names` / `math` axis findings backed by a dropped-superscript quote survive grounding even when the quoted substring is not verbatim in the source.
- [HIGH] Whole-document `partial_ratio` lacks langextract's locality gates. Evidence: our path is substring OR single document-level partial ratio (`grounding.py:49-52`); langextract's tier-3 LCS fuzzy matcher requires coverage ≥ 0.75 **and** matched-token density ≥ 1/3 before accepting `MATCH_FUZZY` (`langextract/00-baseline.md:63`, `resolver.py:57-58`, `1377-1404`). Impact: scattered token overlap across distant regions of a long WG21 paper can score ≥ 0.90 here while langextract would reject or downgrade; no char span is returned to audit where the match landed.
- [MED] Grounding returns boolean keep/drop only; no spans or graded status. Evidence: `ground_spans` returns `(grounded_spans, dropped_count)` (`grounding.py:27-34`, `:56`); `EvidenceSpan` carries `quote`/`axis`/`reason` but no `char_interval` (`models.py:50-60`); sidecar stores quote strings only (`adjudicate.py:266`, `models.py:119`). Impact: inspect/debug cannot show *where* a fuzzy match anchored; upgrading to langextract-style `AlignmentStatus` + char intervals (`langextract/00-baseline.md:66`) is the documented hardening direction without changing the advisory contract.
- [MED] Normalization is symmetric and content-blind by design (OmniDocBench fidelity axis). Evidence: `metrics.py:338-340` (`normalized_text = clean_string(textblock2unicode(text))`); persona 20 prior art (`research/persona/20-math-latex-fidelity.md`) documents the same collapse on Lane 2. Impact: grounding verifies alphanumeric skeleton presence, not delimiter-faithful quotes; table pipe swaps with distinct cell tokens may drop (`| bar | 100 |` vs `| bar | 99 |` → `partial_ratio=0.5455`, grounded=0, reproduced), but swaps that preserve the same normalized token stream still pass.
- [LOW] Empty post-normalize quotes are dropped correctly. Evidence: `grounding.py:45-47` drops when `not norm_quote`; runtime quote `"   |  ^  _  "` → `grounded=0 dropped=1`. Impact: punctuation-only hallucinations fail closed; this is the one robust edge in the current module.

## False-pass hypothesis
The triage model emits `tables: fail, severity: major, confidence: 0.88` with evidence quote `"the following table"` describing a fabricated row swap that never appeared in the markdown. `ground_spans` normalizes both sides (`grounding.py:39-44`), `partial_ratio("thefollowingtable", norm_md)` scores **0.9412** on a typical WG21 paper containing that phrase (`00-baseline.md:76` seeded hypothesis; reproduced 2026-07-03), the span is kept, and because `suggested_verdict != pass` the demotion guard at `adjudicate.py:237-238` does **not** fire (evidence exists). The sidecar records a grounded quote that proves only boilerplate presence, not the claimed table defect.

## False-fail hypothesis
A faithful long verbatim quote with a single OCR glyph substitution outside the normalized alphabet (e.g. an en-dash or smart quote the model copies faithfully but `clean_string` strips differently on haystack vs quote) could fail both substring and partial_ratio if the remaining alnum skeleton diverges enough. Not reproduced on WG21 fixtures this run; the test suite only exercises near-identical long repeats (`test_tapetum_llm.py:87-102`) and obvious hallucinations (`test_tapetum_llm.py:72-85`). Likely rare compared to the confirmed false-pass vectors above.

## What would change my mind
Porting langextract's dual-gate token alignment (monotonic exact DP + LCS with coverage **and** density gates, returning `char_interval` + `AlignmentStatus`) into `ground_spans`, then re-running the 197 sidecars and showing ≥ 90% of current grounded quotes retain exact/lesser status while the `"the following table"` / `$E=mc$`-vs-`$E=mc^2$` probes flip to dropped or `MATCH_FUZZY` demoted to review, would upgrade this to `usable`.
