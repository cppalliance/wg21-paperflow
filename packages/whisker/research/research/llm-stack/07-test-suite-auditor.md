## Findings

- [CRITICAL] No test asserts that `run_agent` forwards authority-doc step budgets to `AgentBackend.run`. Evidence: `runner.py:266-272` omits `max_tokens`/`thinking_budget`; `agents.py:127-133` docstring claims the runner forwards them; `tapetum_llm.md:118-119` and `:137-139` declare Triage `2048/1024` and Adjudicate `4096/4096`; baseline RQ4 (`00-baseline.md:78-79`) names this the smoking gun. `test_tapetum_llm.py:256-257` sets `mock_spec.step.max_output_tokens` and `thinking_budget` on a MagicMock but never inspects `run_agent`/`agent.run` kwargs; `test_runner.py` (415 LOC, 6 test fns) has no budget-forwarding case; `test_capability_validation.py` validates construction-time `agent.thinking_budget` only, not the `run_agent` path. Impact: every tapetum call silently runs at `max_tokens=4096` with thinking off (`adjudicate.py:391`, `model_backends.py:305-309`) and no test would fail if the wiring were fixed or broken again. Owner: `packages/pipeline/tests/test_runner.py`.

## False-pass hypothesis

A tapetum triage returns `verdict=pass`, `confidence=0.92`, and evidence spans that are all hallucinated. `ground_spans` drops every quote; `_custom_decide` keeps `suggested_verdict=pass` because demotion requires `suggested_verdict != VERDICT_PASS` (`adjudicate.py:237-238`). No existing test exercises this path; the suite would stay green.

## False-fail hypothesis

An oversized paper is chunked (`adjudicate.py:203-204` skips tier2); tier1 aggregate lands at `confidence=0.50` (inside the ambiguous band). `test_oversize_chunks_serially_and_skips_tier2` (`test_tapetum_llm.py:710-743`) locks the skip but never asserts the advisory outcome is demoted or flagged as single-tier-only. The lane emits a single-tier verdict where the authority doc promises a two-tier cascade (`tapetum_llm.md:133`, `00-baseline.md:46`), and no test fails on that semantic gap.

## What would change my mind

A failing-then-passing test in `packages/pipeline/tests/test_runner.py` that builds a `_RecordingBackend`/`AgentBackend` pair, dispatches one step with `spec.step.max_output_tokens=2048` and `thinking_budget=1024`, and asserts `backend.calls` or recorded kwargs include those values at the `ModelBackend.run` boundary. Today that test would fail against `runner.py:266-272`, proving the gap is real and guardable.
