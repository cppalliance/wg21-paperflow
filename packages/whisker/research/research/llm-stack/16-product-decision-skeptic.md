# 16 - Product-Decision-Skeptic

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions   The lane delivers real value on RESCUE (9/9 heading-only fails softened) and as a review amplifier (18 `fail` sidecars on table/structure defects whisker already flagged), but it does not solve its stated false-pass mission: PRIMARY delivered value is 1/6, zero permuted-section or table-swap catches on whisker-pass papers, and 92% of compute goes to SECONDARY relitigation.
**Confidence:** high

## Findings
- [CRITICAL] PRIMARY delivered value — the metric for the stated blind spot — is **1 paper out of 6**. Reproduced on all 198 sidecars: among whisker-`pass` papers with risk signals (`adjudicate.py:82-84`, `_has_pass_risk_signals` at `adjudicate.py:97-109`), exactly one (`P4211R0`) received tapetum `review` with a non-heading, non-cosmetic `primary_concern` (mojibake on the wording axis). Zero received `fail`. Zero had `structure` severity `major` or `tables` verdict `fail` with severity `major`. Impact: the lane's raison d'être (`tapetum_llm.md:7-8`, permuted sections / table swaps that survive order-blind `unigram_coverage`) is unmeasured and effectively undelivered on the only population selected to hunt it.
- [CRITICAL] Candidate selection starves the false-pass hunter. Reproduced: `select_candidates` yields PRIMARY=6, SECONDARY=185, RESCUE=9 (200 total; 198 adjudicated). The `unigram_coverage - coverage` gap trigger (`constants.py:59`, `COV_UNIGRAM_GAP_TRIGGER = 0.15`) — the direct proxy for permuted-section false-passes — fired on **0** of the 6 PRIMARY papers. PRIMARY risk breakdown: `lossy_table`=2, `mojibake`=4, `cov_gap`=0. Impact: the selector forwards mojibake and lossy-table markers, not the order-scramble signal named in the product doc; the batch is 92.5% papers whisker already put in `review`.
- [HIGH] On whisker-`pass` papers, tapetum never catches the named defect classes. Reproduced on 6 whisker-pass papers in the batch: `tapetum fail on whisker pass` = 0; `structure major on whisker pass` = 0; `tables fail major on whisker pass` = 0. By contrast, all 18 tapetum `fail` sidecars (`00-baseline.md:45`) had whisker `review` — e.g. `P3977R0`, `P4096R1`, `P4208R0` (tables axis, severity `major`). Impact: the lane amplifies defects whisker already surfaced; it does not close the silent-pass blind spot.
- [HIGH] **75% of tapetum fail/review outcomes relitigate heading/cosmetic noise** the authority doc itself demotes. Reproduced: 93 / 124 sidecars with `suggested_verdict` in `{fail, review}` have heading-only or structure-minor cosmetic `primary_concern` (matching `tapetum_llm.md:55-56`: heading-level jump is `minor`, never `fail`). SECONDARY breakdown: 82 heading rehash vs 30 substantive fail/review out of 183 adjudicated. Impact: most advisory output duplicates or softens signals the deterministic gate already raised, not net-new fidelity findings.
- [HIGH] Lossy-table PRIMARY candidates get heading rehash, not table scrutiny. Evidence: `P3724R3` (whisker `pass`, `lossy_table_count=1`, `data/whisker/p3724r3.whisker.tapetum.json`) → tapetum `review`, `primary_concern` = "heading-level jump (H2 to H4)... cosmetic"; `P3724R4` (same lossy-table signal) → tapetum `pass`, "No significant conversion defects." `tapetum_llm.md:72-76` explicitly assigns row/cell swap detection to the tables axis. Impact: the lane ignores the very risk signal that routed the paper into PRIMARY.
- [MED] The sole PRIMARY "catch" confirms a signal whisker already carried, not a blind spot. `P4211R0`: whisker `pass` with `mojibake_count=16` (no soft flags — mojibake is a selector input, not a gate flag); tapetum `review` citing mojibake on the wording axis. Impact: one unit of delivered value, but it is risk-signal echo, not token-preserving semantic corruption (`tapetum_llm.md:7`) that `unigram_coverage` misses.
- [MED] RESCUE is the one product claim that matches outcomes. Reproduced: 9/9 heading-monotone-only fails (`adjudicate.py:90-92`) received tapetum `pass` or cosmetic `review`, consistent with `tapetum_llm.md:66-68`. Impact: the lane credibly softens false-fail heading noise — a different problem from the false-pass blind spot, but real and aligned with its RESCUE population design.
- [LOW] The two-tier cascade adds no second opinion in production. Evidence: `escalated=true` in 0 / 198 sidecars (`00-baseline.md:46`); `fast` and `deep` both resolve to `alliance-pod` (`tapetum_llm.md:17-19`). Impact: product narrative ("cheap fast model; ambiguous calls escalate to deep") does not match runtime; every paper gets one triage pass regardless of defect severity.

## False-pass hypothesis
A WG21 paper with two H2 sections permuted globally but high `unigram_coverage` (>= 0.95) and orderly reading flow (`coverage` gap < 0.15, no lossy-table or mojibake markers) receives whisker `pass` and is **never forwarded** to tapetum — it fails none of `_has_pass_risk_signals` (`adjudicate.py:97-109`). Even if manually adjudicated, a single triage pass at `confidence` 0.95 (outside `[0.35, 0.65]`, `constants.py:22-23`) would stand with `escalated=false` (`00-baseline.md:46`), matching the observed production band.

## False-fail hypothesis
`N5044` (whisker `review`, tapetum `fail`): the model flags `structure: fail, severity: major` with note "not a C++ standards proposal... travel guide for Brno" — contradicting `tapetum_llm.md:31` ("The document TYPE is never a defect"). A faithful administrative conversion would receive an advisory `fail` on document-type grounds, not conversion fidelity.

## What would change my mind
A labeled holdout of permuted-section / table-row-swap false passes that whisker `pass`es, with tapetum PRIMARY recall >= 50% (>= 5 catches in 10 injected or verified cases), would flip the verdict to `usable` for the stated product claim. RESCUE success alone is insufficient; it solves a different problem.
