# 30 - self-hosted-VLM-feasibility

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions — self-hosted 7B-class VLM judging on RunPod is feasible under model sovereignty, but requires a **second GPU pod** (not colocation on `alliance-pod`), a new `VllmVisionBackend` + multimodal `run_agent` extension, and a general instruct VLM (or judge finetune) rather than olmOCR alone; Opus can validate text-only adjudication today but not page-image QA without pipeline changes.
**Confidence:** high

## False-pass hypothesis

A **Qwen2.5-VL-7B** judge hallucinates table cell text visible on the page image, emits `verdict=pass` with confident `axis_findings`, and fusion treats the VLM lane as independent confirmation — while markdown is wrong but visually "plausible" in the raster. OCR-category hallucination rates ~60% (`05-web.md` Q4) make this likely without DocVAL-style grounding (`05-web.md` Q4, `https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22521v2`) or RaV-IDP re-render checks (`05-web.md` Q5).

## False-fail hypothesis

**olmOCR-2-7B** or **granite-docling-258M** flags conversion defects on faithful markdown because the finetune optimizes for re-transcription (YAML/DocTags) rather than diff-against-markdown judging; a page image + markdown chunk prompt without DOCR-Inspector-style training yields spurious "missing table row" findings when the model reproduces PDF layout differently from `tomd` pipe tables — `05-web.md` Q1 olmOCR card, `00-baseline.md:13-19`.

## What would change my mind

Live **dual-pod** proof: (1) `GET /v1/models` on a new RunPod serving `Qwen/Qwen2.5-VL-7B-Instruct` or `allenai/olmOCR-2-7B-1025-FP8` with documented `--mm-processor-kwargs`, (2) one `VllmVisionBackend` call returning validated whisker `Adjudication` on a WG21 page PNG + markdown chunk, (3) `alliance-pod` unchanged and still serving `deepseek-v4-pro` at `max_num_seqs` 8 without OOM — would flip verdict to **usable** and retire colocation skepticism.
