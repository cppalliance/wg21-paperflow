# 19 - Benchmark-Ground-Truth-Auditor

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions (+ det-first architecture is sound, but our golden blessing process validated photocopied ideals and missed TOC-class defects until post-hoc gates; adopt olmOCR's verified-fact gating pattern on ideals, not its full review-app workflow)
**Confidence:** high

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] **Blessing validated coverage/drift and "not a raw seed," not source-anchored structure; TOC leaks shipped in PR ideals and in committed `p3968r0`.** Evidence: `golden_qa.py:584-630` (`fidelity_verdict` is coarse coverage/drift only; `bless_stem` rejects uncorrected seeds at `:618-622` but does not diff ideals against source structure); `golden-qa-gap/00-baseline.md:36-42` (PR #290 p1122r3, PR #293 p0533r9 passed all pre-MC2 gates); runtime fixtures: `snapshots/p1122r3.md:25` (`## 1. Introduction 3`), `snapshots/p0533r9.md:22` (`CONTENTS`), `p3968r0.golden.md:20` (`Contents` + flat page-numbered TOC block); `score-baseline.json:9,101,262` pins `no_toc_leak: false` on those three committed goldens. Impact: `test_golden_gate.py:24-40` only ratchets axis scores vs `baselines.json` (3 blessed ideals: `baselines.json:1-25`); it never asserts structural invariants on the ideal itself, so validators validated validators that echoed tomd bugs (MC3 photocopy-golden, `golden-qa-gap/00-baseline.md:44-50`).

- [CRITICAL] **Missing pre-merge mechanical check: `whisker --gate fail` (or equivalent `run_gates` on `ideals/<stem>.md`) is not a golden-PR CI hook; it exists only at local `tomd bless` time and was added after `p3968r0` shipped.** Evidence: `bless_stem` calls `_call_whisker_score_file(ideal)` and hard-fails on any gate (`golden_qa.py:623-630`); `gates.py:209-218` wires six hard gates including `no_toc_leak`; `__main__.py:174-178` documents `--gate {pass,review,fail}` CI contract; `test_score_pinning.py:153-155` pins gate outcomes but does not require pass (`research/llm-qa-integration/04-test-suite-auditor.md:8`); `00-baseline.md:61-63` (2026-07-16 re-run: human agreement 4/9→8/9; all four new hard fails from det `no_toc_leak`). Impact: the blessing process is human-in-the-loop without a enforced mechanical ideal invariant at PR time; wiring `uv run --package whisker whisker --gate fail` (or a hermetic `run_gates(ideal_md)` test on every `ideals/*.md` touched in a PR) is the lowest-cost fix.

- [HIGH] **olmOCR human verification is per-fact beside the PDF, not per-document blessing; `checked: verified` is provenance metadata, but `benchmark.py` scores all loaded facts until `rejected` ones are deleted.** Evidence: `review_app.py:26-34` (queue PDFs with `checked is None`); `review_app.py:127-147` (`/update_test` writes arbitrary fields including `checked`); `templates/review.html:406-410,447-452` (stats for null/verified/rejected; Approve sets `checked=verified`, Reject sets `rejected`; PDF.js highlights fact text); `tests.py:36-38,101` (`TestChecked` enum on `BasePDFTest.checked`); `benchmark.py:223-230,143` (`load_tests` loads entire JSONL, no `checked==verified` filter; every test runs); `miners/delete_rejected.py:38-40,193-196` (rejected facts removed before publish, not at score time). Sample-data fraction: 16/99 facts `verified` (16.2%), 83 `null` (`dataset.jsonl` count, 2026-07-16). Impact: olmOCR separates **mining** (LLM/GPT miners under `bench/miners/`, `README.md:218`) from **gating** (deterministic `test.run()`), but unverified mined facts still affect local benchmark runs until rejected; production dataset hygiene is a release step, not pytest.

- [HIGH] **olmOCR is a model for fact-level ground truth + deterministic scoring, not for our whole-document ideal workflow.** Evidence: `00-baseline.md:53-55` ("LLM mines candidate facts, human review app marks `checked: verified`, bench gates only on verified facts" — partially overstated: scorer does not filter verified, deleter does); `05-web.md / Q5 / olmOCR-Bench README` (machine-checkable pass/fail, no LLM judge); our parallel: `golden_qa.py:331-332` (`checked: verified` facts), `facts.py` Lane 3 (`whisker/CLAUDE.md:316-330`), but golden PRs still bless monolithic `.md` ideals without required verified negative facts (e.g. absent `CONTENTS`, absent page-suffixed duplicate headings). Impact: adopt olmOCR's **unit-test facts on ideals** (low cost, ~10 LOC gate already exists as `no_toc_leak`) before adopting its Flask review UI (high cost).

- [MED] **OmniDocBench stage 2 (human char-level correction against source) would have caught our TOC-leak goldens; stage 1 pre-annotation would not; stage 3 CDM render checks are math-specific.** Evidence: `05-web.md / Q3 / OmniDocBench` (stage 1: model pre-annotation; stage 2: human char-level verification; stage 3: expert CDM render checks); our TOC leaks are plain-text chrome (`p3968r0.golden.md:20-34`, `p0533r9.md:22-28`) visible in side-by-side source review, not formula render errors. Impact: stage 2 is the analogue to `tomd review` + human structural edit (`golden/README.md:130-137`); stage 3 is overkill for TOC; stage 1 would propagate TOC from PaddleOCR/LayoutLMv3 into "ground truth" unless stage 2 removes it.

- [MED] **Adoptable mechanisms ranked by cost (low→high).** (1) **CI `run_gates` on touched `ideals/*.md`** — exists (`gates.py`, `bless_stem`); missing only workflow/CI wire-up; ~0 new LOC. (2) **`no_toc_leak` + MC1 front-matter truth soft gate** — `gates.py:143-185`, proposed MC1 `golden-qa-gap/00-baseline.md:25-34`; tens of LOC. (3) **Required `checked: verified` absent/present facts per blessed paper** (olmOCR pattern) — scaffold exists (`golden_qa.py:331-343`, `corpus_tools.py`); authoring cost per paper. (4) **`tomd review` LLM punch-list** — optional today (`golden_qa.py:234-258`, `tomd-review/SKILL.md:23` flags chrome/TOC); cloud model, non-CI. (5) **olmOCR `review_app.py` per-fact UI** — Flask + PDF.js (`review_app.py:251-294`); high build cost, wrong granularity for WG21 full-paper ideals. (6) **OmniDocBench stage 2 char-level pass** — full-time annotator cost. (7) **OmniDocBench stage 3 expert CDM** — researcher hours, math-only ROI.

- [LOW] **`test_golden_gate` guards regression of tomd vs ideal distance, not ideal vs source truth.** Evidence: `test_golden_gate.py:1-8,24-40`; `golden/README.md:97-108` (baseline ratchet). Impact: even a perfect gate on ideals leaves MC3 blind if the ideal is a photocopy of tomd output; facts anchored to source (`golden_qa.py:337` "verified" means needle in source) are the olmOCR-style escape hatch.

## False-pass hypothesis

PR #290-class ideal: human blesses a seed corrected only for wording while TOC chrome remains (`golden-qa-gap/00-baseline.md:46-48`). `fidelity_verdict` passes (vocabulary preserved), `is_unedited_seed` false (byte differs from tomd after cosmetic edits), pre-MC2 gates pass, `test_golden_gate` passes if baseline was recorded from the same leaky ideal. Bench NID/MHS/content_recall all green because candidate and reference share the same TOC bug (MC3).

## False-fail hypothesis

Committed golden `p2040r0` pins `heading_monotone: false` while other gates pass (`score-baseline.json:117-125`). A human-shippable WG21 paper whose ideal legitimately contains an H-level jump would be hard-failed by `heading_monotone` (`gates.py:105-109`) if blessed today under `--gate fail`, even when TOC and content are correct.

## What would change my mind

A golden-PR CI trial on the next 10 ideals: `run_gates(ideal)` + ≥3 `checked: verified` source-anchored facts per paper, with human review of failures only (no LLM gate). If TOC-class defects in new ideals drop to 0% while bless latency stays under 30 minutes per paper, the process fix obviates copying olmOCR's review app wholesale and confirms det-first ground-truth hygiene is sufficient.
