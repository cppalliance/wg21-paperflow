# 11 - Product-Decision-Skeptic

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions (+ deterministic-gates-decide matches the 31-repo pattern and repo invariants; the advisory LLM lane is architecturally correct but overweight for unique defect discovery, and the stated product problem is only half-solved because ground-truth authoring and human review capacity sit outside both lanes)
**Confidence:** high

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] The architecture solves **reproducible mechanical verdicts**, not **defensible conversion correctness end-to-end**. Repo `CLAUDE.md:72-74` demands semantic stability; whisker enforces it on the gate via `gates.py:209-218` + `score.py:136-231` (`_decide` hard-fails only on gate failure or `unigram_coverage < 0.85`, `constants.py:37`). `tapetum_llm/fusion.py:148-170` explicitly forbids the LLM from hard-failing or overriding a det fail. Impact: CI (`whisker --gate`, `whisker/CLAUDE.md:414-418`) is trustworthy and reproducible; "defensible QA on WG21 conversions" still depends on miss-classes MC1-MC5 (`research/golden-qa-gap/00-baseline.md:23-70`) that the det gate historically green-lit.

- [HIGH] Product constraints **correctly force** det-primary + advisory LLM, and the ecosystem agrees. Model sovereignty (`CLAUDE.md:64-70`) and determinism (`CLAUDE.md:72-74`) rule out cloud-LLM gating; whisker mirrors that at `whisker/CLAUDE.md:34-35,475-477`. Across 31 reference repos, **zero** use an LLM quality signal as a mechanical accept gate (`00-baseline.md:43-55`); olmOCR, ParseBench, and Unstructured SCORE all gate on deterministic facts (`05-web.md / Q1`). Industry pattern is cascade: deterministic first, probabilistic last (`05-web.md / Q4 / Deterministic vs LLM-Judge Evals: 2026 Guide`). Impact: the architecture question is answered in our favor; the doubt is ROI of the advisory lane, not lane ordering.

- [HIGH] Fresh PR-rerun evidence (2026-07-16) shows **det gates carry the product**, not the LLM lane. After `no_toc_leak` + TOC prompt rule, det+advisory agreement with human verdicts went **4/9 → 8/9**; **every one of the 4 new hard fails came from the deterministic gate**; the LLM only confirmed and localized (`00-baseline.md:61-63`). Impact: marginal **unique** catch rate from the LLM on the PR replay cohort is **0/4**; the lane's recent win is corroboration and triage text, not verdict authority.

- [HIGH] Documented **unique LLM catches are a single-digit inventory** against a ~4751 LOC advisory stack (21 files under `tapetum_llm/`). Traceable unique hit: PR #293, 31 dropped `constexpr` at conf 0.95, det missed (`research/golden-qa-gap/00-baseline.md:66`, `SYNTHESIS.md:182`). Proven operational value (not unique detection): **44% review-queue shrink** via `llm_clear_soft_review` (`research/hybrid-llm-scoring/SYNTHESIS.md:14`, `fusion.py:192-207`). Counter-evidence: pre-fix fusion **false-pass upgraded** p0957r8 despite missing page-13 content (`packages/whisker/research/tapetum-golden-review-findings-2026-07-14.md:7-31`; fixed at `fusion.py:177-190`). Impact: ~4751 LOC buys queue ergonomics and one celebrated semantic hit; it does not justify itself as a primary defect finder.

- [MED] The LLM lane's **stated blind-spot rationale is undermined by its own selection and calibration gaps**. Token-preserving corruption on clean-pass papers never reaches tapetum under `--review-all` (`whisker/CLAUDE.md:490-492`); full runs are the workaround. Two-tier cascade escalated **0/198** times (`tapetum-golden-review-findings-2026-07-14.md:86-89`). Confidence is anti-calibrated: min 0.90, median 0.95, fails at same band as passes (`tapetum-golden-review-findings-2026-07-14.md:86-89`; `05-web.md / Q2 / Overconfidence in LLM-as-a-Judge`). Impact: the lane cannot reliably close the false-pass class it was built for (table/cell swaps, `research/hybrid-llm-scoring/SYNTHESIS.md:48`) while still flooding operators with high-confidence noise (PR #295 false-positive heading flag, `00-baseline.md:65`).

- [MED] **Fidelity invariant is met on the det path, violated on the advisory narrative layer.** Repo `CLAUDE.md:123-129` requires fail-not-partial and verified citations; det hard-fails are binary (`score.py:217-218`). The LLM lane persists ungrounded `reasoning` (rubriс-bleed on p3556r0, `tapetum-golden-review-findings-2026-07-14.md:39-58`) and once upgraded unsafe passes before `clear_blocked_missing_region` (`fusion.py:177-190`). Impact: merged `combined_verdict` is advisory-only by contract, but operators reading `tapetum-inspect.md` can still ship on narrative that is not mechanically verified.

- [LOW] Integrating the LLM deeper (in-path judge gating, weighted fusion) would **trade one product problem for two worse ones** without labeled calibration. LLM-judge CI treated as hard blockers produces flaky pass/fail (`05-web.md / Q1 / What Happens When LLM-Judge CI Gates Are Treated as Hard Blockers`); temp=0 does not eliminate grader disagreement (`05-web.md / Q2 / Necessary but Not Sufficient`). Whisker already contains the langextract-style grounding pattern (`grounding.py:171-241`, `00-baseline.md:58-59`). Impact: "wrong path" is integrating LLM as gate; "right path with gaps" is more deterministic facts + better goldens, not more LLM authority.

## False-pass hypothesis

A clean **det `pass`** with token-preserving table-cell corruption (row/column swap leaving `unigram_coverage` green) on a paper with no PRIMARY risk signals: under `--review-all` it never enters tapetum (`whisker/CLAUDE.md:490-492`), fusion stays `whisker_only` pass (`fusion.py:133-142`), and Lane 3 facts do not gate because only **5/381** papers have `checked: verified` facts (`whisker/CLAUDE.md:696-697`). Downstream dissect reads the wrong cell. Concrete precedent class: P4182R0 table-neighbor facts catch swaps, but corpus coverage does not scale (`research/golden-qa-gap/SYNTHESIS.md:173-174`).

## False-fail hypothesis

Cosmetic `heading_monotone` gate failure on an otherwise complete paper: det hard-fails (`gates.py:215-216`), but fusion rescues to `review` when the LLM says pass/review (`fusion.py:150-160`, `FUSION_RULE_LLM_RESCUE_HEADING`). This is intentional queue relief, not a product bug, unless an operator treats `whisker --gate` exit 5 as ship-blocking without reading the merged advisory record.

## What would change my mind

A labeled holdout (≥30 papers, human ground truth per conversion defect) showing the advisory LLM lane **uniquely catches >15%** of defects that det gates + verified facts miss, **without** increasing false-pass rate above the det-only baseline, would flip this to "usable" and justify the 4751 LOC as primary QA rather than triage tooling. Absent that, evidence that **golden ideal blessing against source** (not more LLM integration) closes MC1/MC3/MC4 would confirm the real gap is process, not architecture.
