# 21 - Cost-Latency-Accountant

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions (+ det-first gating is orders-of-magnitude cheaper in wall-clock than LLM-as-gate on every CI/corpus sweep; hourly pod billing makes token volume irrelevant but operator time and flaky-CI reruns dominate the LLM-gate bill)
**Confidence:** high (wall-clock ratios are measured on 2026-07-16 golden rerun + live repro); medium on absolute dollars (hourly pod rate is undocumented)

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] **Det lane vs LLM lane on the same 9 golden papers: ~2.7–5.7 s/paper CPU vs 12.6 s/paper GPU-bound serial.** Evidence: 2026-07-16 deterministic golden rerun scored all 9 PR ideals (`agent-transcript 8cc736cd/1a1eb301`, table in final message); live repro on the same staged workspace: **24.6 s** total with `--no-reference` (gates-only fast path, **2.7 s/paper**) and **51.5 s** with default oracle/markitdown (**5.7 s/paper**); LLM advisory serial run **113.5 s** for 9/9 papers at `--concurrency 1` (**12.6 s/paper**, `agent-transcript 8cc736cd/768f9c00`). Impact: even on a sunk-cost 24/7 pod, the LLM lane is **4.6× slower** than gates-only det on the golden cohort; promoting LLM to gate multiplies every PR check and corpus sweep by that factor before retries or judge mitigations.

- [CRITICAL] **Pod economics are hourly uptime, not per-token; run size is a wall-clock question, not a marginal-dollar question.** Evidence: `SERVICES.toml:62-63` ("Runs 24/7; billed per hour of uptime, NOT per token, so run size is not a cost question"); `whisker/CLAUDE.md:521-524` (same). **No $/hour rate** appears in `SERVICES.toml` or `MODELS.md`. Impact: the economic lever for gating is **operator iteration time** and **shared-pod occupancy**, not token invoices; an LLM-as-gate architecture does not save money by being "smarter per call," it spends more clock on the same fixed hourly burn.

- [CRITICAL] **Corpus-scan asymmetry: `no_toc_leak` swept 582 papers in 41.5 s and found 8 converted-paper leaks; full LLM adjudication of 582 papers is 25–177× slower.** Evidence: live repro `run_gates` over `WG21_DATA_DIR/paperstore/*.md`: **582 files, 41.516 s** (**0.071 s/paper**); 2026-07-16 session found **8 real TOC leaks** in converted papers (p3039r1, p3427r3, p3427r4, p3428r3, p3596r0, p3596r1, p4019r1, p4221r0) plus 7 debug-artifact matches = 15 regex hits (`agent-transcript 8cc736cd:306-307`); gate implementation `gates.py:143-185`, wired at `:218`. LLM corpus wall-clock anchors: **381 papers in 692.3 s** at client `--concurrency 32` / server `--max-num-seqs 16` (`research/slots-32-regression/SYNTHESIS.md:11-13`) → **582 papers ≈ 1,057 s (~17.6 min)** by linear extrapolation; serial golden rate **582 × 12.6 s ≈ 7,333 s (~122 min)**. Impact: the MC2 fix that moved human agreement 4/9→8/9 came from **~40 LOC of regex** (`00-baseline.md:61-63`, `golden-qa-gap/SYNTHESIS.md` T1.1); an LLM sweep of the same corpus would cost **17 min to 2 h** to reproduce what det did in **<1 min**, with no guarantee of stable pass/fail (`tapetum_llm.md:29`, `05-web.md / Q2 / Necessary but Not Sufficient`).

- [HIGH] **LLM-as-gate on every golden-PR check (9 papers) with production judge mitigations: ~11–14 min wall per check vs 113.5 s today.** Evidence: baseline advisory run **113.5 s** (`768f9c00`); `05-web.md / Q2 / Judging the Judges` (position-swap debiasing **doubles** judge calls); `05-web.md / Q2 / An Empirical Study of LLM-as-a-Judge` (multi-sample averaging beats greedy zero-variance on human correlation); conservative production stack: **2× position swap × 3 samples = 6×** calls → **113.5 × 6 ≈ 681 s (~11.4 min)** per 9-paper PR gate. Flaky-CI tax: `05-web.md / Q1 / What Happens When LLM-Judge CI Gates` (probabilistic gates → flaky CI, spurious blocks; deterministic assertions first); `05-web.md / Q2 / Necessary but Not Sufficient` (up to **~50%** per-item pass/fail disagreement across 20 identical runs at default temp 1.0; temp=0 reduces but does not eliminate). At **20% full reruns**: **681 × 1.2 ≈ 817 s (~13.6 min)** per PR check. Impact: golden-ideal review cadence (9 papers, multiple PRs/week) becomes **tens of minutes of pod-bound wall per PR** instead of **<2 min det + optional advisory**.

- [HIGH] **Full corpus re-score (582 papers) as LLM gate: ~1.8–12.2 h per sweep before flaky reruns; det stays sub-minute to ~1 min.** Evidence: extrapolated optimal tapetum batch **~17.6 min** (`692.3 s × 582/381`); serial golden **~122 min**; with **6× judge mitigation** → **~105 min (~1.8 h)** optimal to **~12.2 h** serial. Measured det full-corpus gate sweep **41.5 s** (this session). Prior full advisory runs: **200 papers in 2,159.8 s (36 min)** at `--concurrency 3` (`packages/whisker/research/llm-batching/00-baseline.md:12-14`, **10.8 s/paper effective**). Tier-2 escalation adds second calls on **13/197** papers post-hardening (`00-baseline.md:23-24`); PDF lane adds page escalations (`pdf_judge.py:287-310`, `constants.py:154`, `MAX_PAGE_ESCALATIONS`). Impact: nightly or pre-release corpus gates are **routine at det speed** but **operator-blocking at LLM-gate speed**; every prompt/gate change forces a multi-hour revalidation tax.

- [HIGH] **Flaky-CI tax is an economic line item LLM-as-gate cannot amortize away.** Evidence: `05-web.md / Q1 / What Happens When LLM-Judge CI Gates` (same code passes then fails; teams absorb reruns + spurious blocks); `05-web.md / Q2 / Overconfidence in LLM-as-a-Judge` (high confidence, low correctness, dangerous for gating). Whisker already pins temp/seed client-side (`02-determinism-auditor.md:14`, `model_backends.py:310-312`) but hosted vLLM is not bit-stable (`tapetum_llm.md:29`). Impact: det gate reruns are **free CPU milliseconds**; LLM-gate reruns are **full adjudication wall** on a shared pod, and disputed blocks become **human triage hours** (the hidden cost `05-web.md / Q4 / Deterministic vs LLM-Judge Evals 2026` assigns to probabilistic layers).

- [MED] **Industry cascade economics match our split: deterministic first at sub-ms cost, LLM last for semantic remainder.** Evidence: `05-web.md / Q4 / Deterministic vs LLM-Judge Evals 2026` ("deterministic gates catch 30-60% of failures at sub-ms cost"); `05-web.md / Q4 / AI Guardrails And Output Validation 2026` ("LLM judge on every response is a flagged v1 anti-pattern"). Whisker implements this: **219 LOC** `gates.py` decides (`00-baseline.md:63`); **~4,751 LOC** `tapetum_llm/` advises. Impact: moving LLM from advisory to gate inverts the recommended cost pyramid and contradicts 0/31 repo precedent (`00-baseline.md:43-44`).

- [LOW] **Escalation and JSON-retry overhead are small today but become mandatory spend under LLM-gate.** Evidence: **1 model retry total** on 200-paper run (`llm-batching/00-baseline.md:14-15`); **0/198** tier-2 escalations on confidence band (`constants.py:23-26`, `tapetum-golden-review-findings-2026-07-14.md:86-89`); `model_backends.py` bounded JSON retries (`06-error-handling-robustness.md:20`). Under LLM-as-gate, position-swap + multi-sample mitigations dominate cost (`05-web.md / Q2`), not these tails. Impact: current retry budget is negligible vs a **6×** judge multiplier.

## False-pass hypothesis

A TOC-leaked paper ships because an LLM-as-gate run flips `fail→pass` on rerun variance while det `no_toc_leak` would have blocked deterministically: p1122r3 and p0533r9 both hard-fail on the gate (`agent-transcript 1a1eb301` table) but pre-fix LLM false-cleared p1122r3 at pass/0.98 (`00-baseline.md:82`, `whisker/CLAUDE.md:505-511`). Paying **6× judge wall** per PR does not remove that flip risk (`05-web.md / Q2`); it buys sampling, not mechanical authority.

## False-fail hypothesis

Nine heading-monotone-only cosmetic fails (RESCUE population, `fusion.py:151-160`) become **blocking CI noise** if LLM-as-gate inherits det's heading gate or if judge variance spuriously fails faithful reflows: **9/14** hard fails are heading-monotone with uni≥0.947 (`research/persona/24-false-positive-hunter.md`). Det false-fail is **stable and cheap**; LLM-gate false-fail triggers **681 s+ re-runs** and reviewer thrash (`05-web.md / Q1`).

## What would change my mind

A measured A/B on the **582-paper corpus** showing an LLM-as-gate stack (position-swap + 3-sample average + pinned open-weight model) matches det `no_toc_leak` **recall on all 8 known leaks** AND heading/TOC precision vs human labels, with **≤2% run-to-run verdict flip** across 10 back-to-back full-corpus sweeps, while total wall-clock stays **≤2×** the **692.3 s / 381-paper** optimal tapetum batch rate (~**35 min** for 582). Until that exists, economics favor det-decides + optional advisory LLM.
