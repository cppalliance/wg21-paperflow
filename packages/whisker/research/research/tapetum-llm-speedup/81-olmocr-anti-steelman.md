# 81 - olmocr-Anti-Steelman

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions — olmOCR proves dense 7B page-parallel *extraction* can saturate an H100, but its headline throughput is not transferable to our fail-closed MoE *verification* fleet without abandoning the fidelity and determinism rules that define "same quality."
**Confidence:** high

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] **Workload classes are incomparable: olmOCR = 1 VLM call/page to emit markdown; tapetum-llm = ~6 serial judge calls/paper with grounded structured output.** olmOCR sends one multimodal user message per page (`pipeline.py:133-146`), cap `max_tokens=8000`, `MODEL_MAX_CONTEXT=16384` (`pipeline.py:107,162`). We run monolith → metadata → 0–5 page escalations (`pdf_judge.py:753`) → 0–5 unit checks (`unit_judge.py:389`) = **6.0 calls/paper**, **2284 calls/381 papers** (`00-baseline.md:22-23`), each on **deepseek-v4-pro** at **393216** context (`SERVICES.toml:68-69`), with **full candidate markdown resent on every unit check** (`unit_judge.py:769-776`). Impact: olmOCR's 1288 pages in 307 s (`05-web.md:38-39`) is an extraction benchmark; naïvely equating it to our cold **3003 s** run would ignore ~5× call volume and orders-of-magnitude prefill inflation. Quality risk if ignored: shipping a 5–10 min plan that cuts calls without proving recall on the 16/381 verdict-changing papers (`00-baseline.md:29-30`).

- [CRITICAL] **Latency math closes only at the fleet layer; per-call economics diverge ~5× before call-count is counted.** Our implied per-call latency at 16 server slots: **~20 s** (`00-baseline.md:24-25`), giving **~120 s serial wall per 6-call paper** regardless of `_DEFAULT_CONCURRENCY=32` cross-paper slots (`00-baseline.md:38-39`, `cli.py:125`). olmOCR's published batch: **1288 pages / 307 s = 0.24 s/page amortized** and **3050 output tok/s aggregate** (`05-web.md:38-39`). Re-applying our slot model to olmOCR's call census: **307 s × 16 slots / 1288 calls ≈ 3.8 s/call** (same formula as `00-baseline.md:25`). Ratio **20 s / 3.8 s ≈ 5.3× per call**, times **6 calls/paper ≈ 32× more judge work** before parallelism differences. Impact: even a perfect olmOCR-style page-parallel scheduler cannot reach **300 s** on our task without call elimination or a dense cascade; copying concurrency alone saves **0 s** on the serial-within-paper chain. Quality risk: none from math alone; risk is treating amortized **0.24 s/page** as achievable per-call latency.

- [HIGH] **olmOCR speed enablers we CANNOT copy: temperature-ramp retries (D5), truncation tolerance, pdftotext fallback, partial-document acceptance.** Retries index `TEMPERATURE_BY_ATTEMPT = [0.1 … 1.0]` per attempt (`pipeline.py:85,164-165`), up to **`max_page_retries=8`** (`pipeline.py:1221,289`). Non-`stop` finish or over-context responses set `is_valid=False` but are still returned and can succeed after retry (`pipeline.py:205-206,366-369`). Exhausted pages become **`make_fallback_result` pdftotext** with `is_valid=True, is_fallback=True` (`pipeline.py:233-249,375`). Documents ship with up to **`max_page_error_rate=0.004`** (1/250) fallback pages (`pipeline.py:1222,559-567`). We fail closed on escalation errors (`pdf_judge.py:761-763`), cap at review on incomplete coverage (`constants.py:223-227`), and forbid per-call temperature overrides (`00-baseline.md:86`). Impact: adopting olmOCR's retry/fallback stack might cut wall by hiding failures (12% measured retry rate, `05-web.md:41`) but would recreate false-pass paths on table/VLM failures. Quality risk: **HIGH** — silent degradation of fail-closed semantics.

- [HIGH] **olmOCR's in-paper parallelism (all pages via `asyncio.TaskGroup`) and `--max_concurrent_requests 1600` violate our within-paper serial invariant and MoE batch-variance budget.** Every page in a PDF launches concurrently (`pipeline.py:548-551`); global semaphore default **1600** (`pipeline.py:88,1224,1289`). We serial monolith → metadata → escalations → unit checks (`00-baseline.md:36-42`; loops at `pdf_judge.py:753`, `unit_judge.py:389`). Measured: raising server `--max-num-seqs` 16→32 **+60% wall** on our pod (`00-baseline.md:82`). Impact: olmOCR-style page-parallel verification would need an explicit D11 exemption and MoE variance acceptance; unlikely to reproduce their **0.24 s/page** under DeepSeek-class limits. Quality risk: verdict drift from concurrent in-flight requests (why D11 exists).

- [HIGH] **If olmOCR's stack had to verify 381 papers with grounded evidence, production inference would break; their quality path is offline deterministic bench, not runtime LLM judging.** Score-time loop is **`test.run(md_content)`** only — no PDF reread, no judge model (`benchmark.py:116-117`; same in training rewards `grpo_train.py:644-645`). ~**7010** pre-authored JSONL facts (~7k cited in prior scans) are human/LLM-mined offline; runtime adds **zero** verification LLM calls. Mapping our task onto their inference pipeline would require structured pass/fail + quote grounding (we use Pydantic `output_type`, D6/D10) while they **avoid guided JSON at inference** and YAML-parse with retries (`05-web.md:47-48`, `pipeline.py:210-212`). Impact: cold **3003 s** would become **≥381 × (pages × judge latency)** if translated naïvely to per-page VLM QA, or shift cost to offline fact authoring (months, not minutes). Quality risk: olmOCR bench false-clears on secno headings and fuzzy table cells (`tests.py:311-326,395-396`) — the opposite of our golden-contract goals.

- [MED] **Payload asymmetry: olmOCR prefills one page PNG + fixed conversion prompt; we prefill up to 393k tokens of text repeatedly.** Page query is text prompt + one `image_url` (`pipeline.py:138-141`); context ceiling **16384** (`pipeline.py:162,1255`). Each of our ~6 calls embeds **CONVERSION_CONTRACT (~3.7k chars)** plus a fresh `guard_instruction(SRC{secrets.token_hex(4)})` breaking prefix identity (`00-baseline.md:48-52`; tags at `pdf_judge.py:638`, `unit_judge.py:767`). Unit checks include **entire** `candidate_md` (`unit_judge.py:18,775-776`). Impact: olmOCR benefits from short, identical system prefixes per model family; we forfeit prefix-cache hits on every call. Scoped payload (lever 2 in `00-baseline.md:92-94`) is portable; olmOCR's image-only prefill is not. Quality risk: low if scoping is done carefully; high if "send less" becomes "send wrong slice."

- [MED] **What we already do better for a verification fleet: incremental skip, deterministic serial chain, fail-closed audit trail.** Warm rerun **64.8 s** with **375/381 skipped** via SHA-256 fingerprint of markdown, source, prompt, schema, model, `_LANE_VERSION` (`00-baseline.md:19-20`; `_compute_fingerprint` `cli.py:566-591`, skip gate `cli.py:1183-1202`). olmOCR resume skips completed **work items** (`work_queue.py` pattern, cited in prior scans) but has no equivalent "same inputs → skip 6-call judge cascade." We persist dispositions, cap incomplete coverage at review, and raise on failed escalation (`pdf_judge.py:761-763`; `constants.py:223-227`). olmOCR asserts `all(page_result.is_valid)` then still builds documents with fallback pages (`pipeline.py:555-567`). Impact: our warm path already beats any olmOCR-mapped cold path for steady-state ops; their cold extraction record does not beat our **3003 s** when task parity is enforced. Quality risk: none — these are advantages.

## False-pass hypothesis

After eight VLM attempts with temperature ramped to **1.0** (`pipeline.py:85,164-165`), olmOCR accepts **`make_fallback_result` pdftotext** as `is_valid=True` (`pipeline.py:233-249,375`) and still concatenates the page into the Dolma doc when fallback rate ≤ **0.004** (`pipeline.py:559-567`). Ported to tapetum verification: a timed-out table page would **false-pass** as long as poppler text overlaps token multiset, while visual/table structure defects (the class our unit checks target) remain undetected — the same failure mode flagged for VLM QA in `20-olmocr-pipeline-analyst.md`.

## False-fail hypothesis

If we copied olmOCR's **`finish_reason != "stop"` ⇒ `is_valid=False`** retry loop (`pipeline.py:205-206`) onto our **1536-token-capped** unit checks (`00-baseline.md:27-28`) without olmOCR's truncation-forgiving downstream acceptance, legitimately complete structured verdicts that hit length stop tokens would **false-fail** or burn retry budget, inflating wall toward **`UNIT_CHECK_TIMEOUT_SECONDS=120`** (`constants.py:241`) per slot.

## What would change my mind

A cold benchmark on **our** 381-paper corpus where olmOCR-style infrastructure (dense 7B or 32B judge, scoped payloads, in-paper parallel allowed) runs the **same** 6-stage grounded verification prompts and matches tapetum sidecar verdicts on the **16/381** LLM-changing papers (`00-baseline.md:29-30`) in **≤600 s** without temperature ramps, pdftotext fallback, or sub-**0.004** partial-page discard — no such path exists in `olmocr/pipeline.py` or `olmocr/bench/` today (`pipeline.py:139` always calls `build_no_anchoring_v4_yaml_prompt()` for extraction).
