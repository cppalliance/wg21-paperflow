# 16 - Monolith-Redundancy-Skeptic

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions — the PDF monolith is ~17% of cold-fleet wall but redundant on 177/181 PDF papers for verdict-changing findings; safe to replace with screen + metadata + units plus a cheap reorder-only call, not to delete outright without that substitute.
**Confidence:** high (381 sidecars measured 2026-07-23; file:line on prompts and folding)

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] **Monolith is the largest single prefill but only ~16–17% of fleet wall, insufficient alone for 5–10 min.** Census: **381 first-pass calls** = **180 PDF monolith** + **201 HTML tier-1** (`10-call-graph-accountant.md:9-11`). At ~20 s/call and 16 slots: **381×20/16 ≈ 476 s (~15.9% of 3003 s)** (`00-baseline.md:22-25`). Monolith sends **full PDF text + full markdown** (`pdf_judge.py:640-643`); unit checks repeat full markdown per call (`unit_judge.py:769-776`). Prefill is ~2× a unit call (~14k vs ~7k tok, `research/llm-golden-verification-gap/personas/c21-cost-realist.md:10`). Impact: dropping monolith saves **~476–600 s** on a cold run; **still ~2400 s short** of 300–600 s target without unit-call cuts (`10-call-graph-accountant.md:16-20`).

- [CRITICAL] **Surviving monolith quotes rarely drive verdicts the downstream cascade does not already reach.** On **181 PDF sidecars**: **392 monolith quotes emitted**, **87 (22.2%)** survive grounding as `candidate_not_found`, **228 (58.2%)** refuted `present_in_candidate`, **77 ambiguous**. **39 papers** have ≥1 surviving monolith cnf; **23** also have unit cnf (overlap). **Only 4 papers** would change verdict if monolith cnf vanished and metadata/units/escalations unchanged: **3 review→pass** (N5036, P3828R1, P4124R0 — sole monolith cnf, meta pass, zero defect groups) and **1 fail→review** (P4048R0 — mono cnf on `"6.4 Design Team"`, meta review only). Impact: **97.8% PDF verdict stability** on removal; quality risk **LOW for fleet pass/review mix**, **MEDIUM for P4048R0-class mono-only fails**.

- [HIGH] **Page screen + escalations subsume the historical monolith false-pass, not the monolith itself.** `PAGE_JUDGE_SYSTEM_PROMPT` explicitly excludes document-wide reordering (`pdf_judge.py:225-226`); monolith prompt includes reordering (`pdf_judge.py:190-196`). Per-page screen calibrated on p0957r8: page-13 recall **0.8810 < PAGE_RECALL_FLOOR=0.90** while doc recall stays above **PDF_JUDGE_RECALL_FLOOR=0.85** (`constants.py:164-177`). Fleet: **18 flagged pages / 14 papers**, **16 escalation calls**, **2 confirmed** `content_missing`; **0** papers with confirmed escalation and zero monolith cnf. p0957r8 is absent from this sidecar set; design intent is screen catches localized loss monolith missed (`tapetum_llm.md:93`). Impact: screen+escalation replaces monolith for **localized absence**; monolith's remaining unique scope is **global reorder/structure**.

- [HIGH] **Conditional monolith on existing screen/risk triggers is a no-op; 180/181 PDF papers would still call.** `screen_pages` runs before monolith (`pdf_judge.py:636-646`). Measured: **180/181 PDF** have ≥1 `risk_signal` (router always fires); **14/181** have screen-flagged pages; union **180/181**. Only **1 PDF** would skip monolith under "flagged OR risk" gating. Alternative gate "no screen flags AND doc recall≥0.85": **165/181** eligible to skip, but **34 of those** have monolith cnf the screen missed (localized loss hidden in doc-average recall). Impact: naive conditional saves **~16 calls (~21 s wall)**; aggressive conditional (clean screen) saves **~165 calls (~206 s wall)** but risks **34 papers** including **16 with mono cnf and zero unit cnf**.

- [HIGH] **Downstream paths catch defects monolith misses more often than the reverse.** **58/181 PDF** papers have **zero monolith dispositions** yet metadata review/fail or unit defect groups (`pdf_judge.py:675-890`). **18 papers** have defect groups with **zero monolith cnf** (e.g. P1000R8: monolith pass-leaning reasoning, unit `table_corruption` — `14-steelman.md:12`). **63/181** papers emitted monolith quotes **all refuted/ambiguous** (full prefill, zero cnf). **79/181** had no monolith quotes at all. **31 PDF fails** driven by metadata `fail` with **14 having zero cnf from any path**. Impact: monolith is a weak primary pass; metadata + routed units are the verdict workhorses. Quality risk of removal is **downstream under-coverage on 16 mono-only-cn papers**, not loss of monolith-only detection breadth.

- [MED] **Document-wide reordering — monolith's stated unique value — has zero measured fleet hits.** Prompt requires flagging "reordering" (`pdf_judge.py:190-196`); page judge abdicates it (`pdf_judge.py:225-226`). Sidecar `reasoning` mentions "reorder" on **6/181 PDF** papers; all are negations ("no reordering found") except TOC-leak structure notes. No sidecar cites reordering as the deciding defect. Prior art rejected 33× per-page LLM in favor of screen+escalation for cost (`research/llm-golden-verification-gap/archaeology/a05-per-page-judging.md:8`). Impact: a **cheap reorder-only call** (outline headings vs source outline, ~60-word output, same output discipline `pdf_judge.py:203-212`) could replace ~90% of monolith prefill while preserving the one defect class units/screen cannot see.

- [MED] **Replace monolith with screen + cheap summary is the viable architecture; pure deletion is not.** Proposed shape: keep deterministic `screen_pages` (`pdf_judge.py:296-321`) → mandatory metadata (`pdf_judge.py:675-684`) → routed units (`pdf_judge.py:880-890`); add **one small LLM call** only when (a) any page flagged, OR (b) doc recall ∈ [0.85, 0.90), OR (c) metadata outline drift — sending **source outline + heading list + md outline** (~2–3k tok) asking reorder/leak/TOC only. LLMTrace/SAJA cascade precedent: cheap gate first, expensive judge on flags (`05-web.md:91-99`). Measured trigger on (a|b|c): **~20–30 papers**, not 181. Impact: **~150–160 monolith calls eliminated (~200–400 s wall)** plus largest prefill reduction on remaining calls; quality risk **LOW** if reorder substitute runs on borderline papers.

- [LOW] **HTML tier-1 (201 calls) is a parallel monolith with even lower marginal cnf yield.** HTML sidecars: **105/197** with dispositions, **10 total cnf quotes** vs PDF **87 cnf**. Same serial cascade architecture (`adjudicate.py:730-798`, `10-call-graph-accountant.md:13-14`). PDF findings transfer; HTML monolith cuts are **more aggressive** but **orthogonal** to PDF persona scope.

## False-pass hypothesis

Remove monolith on **165 "clean screen" papers** without a reorder substitute: **P4048R0** drops **fail→review** (sole cnf `"6.4 Design Team"`, meta review, zero units). A future paper with **faithful per-page recall (≥0.90 each page) but swapped section order** (e.g. §5 before §3) would **pass** screen, metadata, and units — only the monolith prompt explicitly checks reordering (`pdf_judge.py:190-196`); **0 fleet examples today**, but structurally exposed.

## False-fail hypothesis

Keep monolith on every paper: **63 papers** pay full PDF+MD prefill for quotes **100% refuted** by `classify_candidate_evidence` (`pdf_judge.py:697-710`, `_fold_monolith_verdict` `343-367`). **516 present_in_candidate dispositions fleet-wide** (`14-steelman.md:32`) show monolith generates missing-content noise corrected downstream — wasted prefill/decode, not extra safety.

## What would change my mind

Holdout replay of **≥30 labeled PDFs** where verified defect class is **document-wide reordering or cross-page structure** (not localized omission): if **≥50%** are caught only by monolith and by neither screen+units nor a cheap outline-only call, monolith stays mandatory. Absent that, measured **4-paper verdict delta** on full removal is the ceiling of monolith marginal value.
