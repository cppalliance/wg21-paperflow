# 20 - In-Paper-Parallelist

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions (+ in-paper parallelism shortens single-paper and `--all-pages` latency but cannot materially shrink the 381-paper cold fleet wall because total LLM call count and 16 server slots are the binding constraint; MoE batch-composition variance makes parallel bursts a quality-stability regression risk unless measured under batch-invariant or serial pod settings)
**Confidence:** high

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] Per-paper LLM calls have almost no *prompt* dependencies; only verdict *folding* is serial. Monolith inputs are `pdf_text` + `tomd_md` (`pdf_judge.py:640-657`). Metadata inputs are `page_units[0].text` + heading outline from `extract_page_units(source_path)` (`pdf_judge.py:662-684`, `unit_judge.py:231-256`), which needs only the PDF path, not monolith output. Page escalations are keyed off deterministic `screen_pages(cleaned_pages, tomd_md)` computed before monolith (`pdf_judge.py:636`, `753-759`). Unit checks are keyed off deterministic `route_pdf_units(page_units, raw_tomd_md)` (`pdf_judge.py:851`, `source_router.py:167+`) with per-unit source packets from `unit_text_map` (`unit_judge.py:389-416`). Escalations do not read monolith judgment; units do not read monolith, metadata, or escalation outputs. Impact: after a shared CPU prep phase (`extract_page_units`, `screen_pages`, `route_pdf_units`), all LLM calls for one paper could run in one parallel wave; serial order in `pdf_judge.py:646-890` and `unit_judge.py:389-423` is scheduling policy, not data necessity. Quality risk: none from dependency analysis alone; verdict merge stays serial (`pdf_judge.py:710-731`, `927-932`).

- [CRITICAL] Max-parallel dependency graph and critical path. **Prep (CPU, serial):** text-layer extract → `screen_pages` → `extract_page_units` → `route_pdf_units`. **Parallel LLM wave (width W):** monolith + metadata + up to `MAX_PAGE_ESCALATIONS` page calls + up to `MAX_UNIT_CHECKS` unit calls (`constants.py:191,223`). Fleet routed mode: W ≤ 2 + 5 + 5 = **12**. `--all-pages` mode: W ≤ 2 + 5 + page_count (e.g. 15-page paper → W=22). **Post (CPU, serial):** `ground_spans` / `classify_candidate_evidence`, `verify_unit_evidence`, verdict fold. Critical path length ≈ **1 × ~20 s decode** (00-baseline:24) + prep/post CPU ≈ **20–30 s** per paper vs current serial **~120 s** for a median 6-call paper (00-baseline:38-39). Impact: ~4–6× single-paper wall reduction in theory; **0× reduction in fleet slot-seconds** because call count unchanged (~2284 total, 00-baseline:22-23).

- [CRITICAL] Fleet wall is calls × latency / slots; in-paper parallelism does not reduce total calls. Model: 2284 calls × ~20 s / 16 slots ≈ **2855 s** (00-baseline:23-25), closing on measured **3003 s**. Cross-paper concurrency already saturates the pod: `_DEFAULT_CONCURRENCY = 32` with comment "overfills the pod's 16 scheduler slots" (`cli.py:119-127`), `asyncio.Semaphore(concurrency)` + `asyncio.gather` at `cli.py:1112-1113`, `1397-1399`. Each paper currently emits **one** in-flight LLM request at a time (`pdf_judge.py:753` serial escalation loop, `unit_judge.py:389` serial unit loop; `judge_task.py:10-15` bypasses pipeline `Semaphore(1)` but does not add in-paper fan-out). Total slot-seconds = **45,680** regardless of whether those calls are serial or parallel within papers. Impact: **~0 s expected saving on the 3003 s cold fleet** (± queueing noise); lever is useless for the 5–10 min fleet target unless paired with call elimination or more slots/pods.

- [HIGH] In-paper parallelism helps only single-paper / tail regimes, not steady-state fleet throughput. **Single-paper `--all-pages`:** timeout budget already assumes serial unit depth: `_pdf_judge_timeout_seconds(unit_count)` scales `(1 + unit_count) × UNIT_CHECK_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (`cli.py:154-161`); a 15-page paper at serial 15 × ~20 s ≈ **300 s** of unit-check wall alone vs parallel ceil(15/16) × ~20 s ≈ **40 s**. **Fleet tail:** when remaining global calls < 16, slots idle; parallel per-paper reduces depth per finishing paper, trimming tail latency by at most **~1–3%** of 3003 s (~30–90 s) for 381 papers, not the 25× gap to 5–10 min. Impact: adopt in-paper parallel for `--all-pages` / `--inspect` UX; do not expect fleet cold-run breakthrough.

- [HIGH] MoE batch-composition variance is real for verdict stability, and in-paper parallelism worsens the exposure. Production judge is **deepseek-v4-pro MoE** at **`--max-num-seqs 16`** (00-baseline:65-67,83). `MODELS.md:69-70` documents MoE expert routing sensitivity to concurrent batch composition; `MODELS.md:77-78` notes hosted vLLM is not batch-invariant without server flags. Measured **≥25% verdict-flip rate** on identical reruns at paper-level c=32 (`whisker/CLAUDE.md` advisory section, `research/llm-golden-verification-gap/personas/c13-determinism.md:8`). In-paper parallel bursts would co-schedule heterogeneous prompts on the same pod (monolith full-doc prefill ~6× per paper per 00-baseline:46-47, page escalation, unit check with full `candidate_md` per call at `unit_judge.py:775-776`) increasing batch token-shape diversity versus today's one-call-at-a-time per paper. Impact: wall-clock unchanged on fleet; **quality-stability risk is negative EV** for the speedup study's "same findings" bar unless rerun under controlled serial or batch-invariant infra.

- [MED] Advisory lane is correctly exempt from D11; in-paper parallel is a whisker-local scheduling change only. `judge_task.py:17-23` implements the sanctioned per-package mechanism; `whisker/CLAUDE.md` states cross-paper concurrency up to 32 with in-paper serial today. Parallelizing unit/escalation loops inside `judge_pdf_extraction` / `run_unit_checks` does not touch `pipeline.runner._parallel_semaphore` (`MODELS.md:59-60`). Impact: implementation is bounded to whisker; no dissect regression. Quality risk: MoE variance (above), not D11 violation.

- [MED] Prefix-cache interaction under parallel bursts may *increase* per-call latency, partially offsetting single-paper wins. Shared `CONVERSION_CONTRACT` prefix is broken by per-call random `guard_instruction(tag)` at the system-prompt tail (`00-baseline:48-52`, `unit_judge.py:767-768`, `pdf_judge.py:638-639`). 05-web.md Q1/Q4: APC/prefix gains require identical leading tokens; concurrent diverse prefills on MoE at c=16 already showed 0%→74.3% prefix hits after vLLM fix under cross-paper load. Parallel within-paper multiplies simultaneous full-markdown prefills (`unit_judge.py:775-776`), competing for the same 16 KV blocks. Impact: single-paper latency win may be **< linear in W**; fleet wall unchanged or slightly worse if ITL inflates.

- [LOW] CPU work currently sits inside the paper semaphore and blocks cross-paper slot recycling slightly. `extract_page_units` runs between monolith and metadata while the paper holds `async with sem` (`cli.py:1120`, `pdf_judge.py:662-668`). Moving prep before the parallel LLM wave frees no server slots but reduces paper-slot hold time by seconds on large PDFs. Impact: **≪1%** fleet wall; orthogonal to in-paper LLM parallel.

## False-pass hypothesis

Parallelizing monolith with five unit checks on a borderline paper: monolith draw routes to a "pass" expert composition under a fat heterogeneous batch while a serial rerun would have demoted to review on the same greedy decode; fusion still cannot promote to deterministic pass, but the advisory `suggested_verdict` silently clears a paper the operator treats as LLM-verified (`whisker/CLAUDE.md`: ≥25% flip baseline). Concrete trigger: `--concurrency 32` fleet + new in-paper W=12 burst on `deepseek-v4-pro` without batch-invariant kernels (`MODELS.md:77-78`).

## False-fail hypothesis

A paper in `--all-pages` mode fires page_count parallel unit checks; several hit `UNIT_CHECK_TIMEOUT_SECONDS = 120` (`constants.py:197`) under slot contention, land in `failed_unit_ids`, and force `coverage_complete=false` → advisory review cap (`unit_judge.py:419-423`, `pdf_judge.py:895-902`) on an otherwise clean conversion. Serial execution at c=1 would have succeeded sequentially without timeout.

## What would change my mind

A measured A/B on the live 381-paper cold fleet: **(A)** current serial in-paper + c=32 vs **(B)** max-parallel in-paper + c=32 (same 2284 calls, same model), reporting fleet wall within **±2%** AND defect-group recall / merged-verdict distribution within the established MoE drift band (≤2 pp flip on the 16 papers whose merged verdict changed in 00-baseline:29-30), would upgrade fleet utility from "~0 s" to "material." Without that, the slot accounting stands.
