# 18 - Escalation-Overlap-Auditor

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions (skip-and-synthesize dedupe is safe on measured overlap, but saves only ~23 s fleet wall on the 3003 s run; not a primary 5–10 min lever without bundling larger call cuts)
**Confidence:** high

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] The same PDF page can receive BOTH an escalation call and a unit check in one run: `judge_pdf_extraction` runs `_escalate_page` in a serial loop (`pdf_judge.py:753-832`) and then `run_unit_checks` in a separate serial loop (`pdf_judge.py:870-890`) with no dedupe guard. Sidecar census (381 files under `data/whisker/llm/*.tapetum.json`, PDF lane n=181): **18/18 escalation pages (100%) also appear in `unit_checks`**; **0 escalation-only pages**; 764 unit-checked pages had no escalation. Impact: eliminable **18 LLM calls** (~0.8% of ~2284 fleet calls); fleet wall **~22.5 s** saved (18 × ~20 s / 16 slots, per 00-baseline latency model); up to **~100 s serial** saved on a single paper with 5 overlapping pages. Quality risk: low if escalation is skipped only when a unit check is already scheduled and `page_escalations` is synthesized from unit evidence (see finding 4).

- [HIGH] Both mechanisms share the same low-recall trigger threshold but serve different roles today. Deterministic screen: `screen_pages()` flags when `content_recall(tomd_md, dehyphenated_page) < PAGE_RECALL_FLOOR` (`pdf_judge.py:296-320`, `constants.py:177`). Risk router: `route_pdf_units()` emits `low_recall` when `content_recall(candidate_md, unit.text) < PAGE_RECALL_FLOOR` (`source_router.py:209-222`). On the cold fleet, every screen-flagged page (18) also had a router `low_recall` signal and was unit-checked; **261 additional `low_recall` router signals** were HTML `section:*` units (not PDF pages). Impact: PDF low-recall is fully double-called; unifying PDF low-recall does not affect HTML lane.

- [HIGH] Prompt overlap is partial, not redundant. `PAGE_JUDGE_SYSTEM_PROMPT` (`pdf_judge.py:215-250`) asks a binary scoped question: is content-bearing text from THIS page missing (`content_missing` + verbatim `missing_content` quotes; schema `PageJudgment` at `models.py:191-228`). `UNIT_CHECK_SYSTEM_PROMPT` (`unit_judge.py:129-159`) asks for faithful preservation with **10 typed defect classes** (`content_omission`, `table_corruption`, `qualifier_omission`, etc.) and pass/review/fail verdict semantics (`UnitCheck`). Both embed `CONVERSION_CONTRACT` (`unit_judge.py:70-106`) and send **full candidate markdown** per call (`pdf_judge.py:394-395`, `unit_judge.py:775-776`). Impact: merging into one multi-task prompt risks double-digit rubric degradation (RuVerBench caution in `05-web.md` Q5); **skip-and-synthesize** is safer than prompt fusion.

- [HIGH] On overlapping pages, unit checks are strictly richer than escalations for defect detection. Of 18 overlap pages: escalation `content_missing=true` (confirmed) on **2**; escalation sanctioned (`content_missing=false`) on **16**; unit verdicts on the same pages: **10 pass, 3 review, 5 fail**. The 2 confirmed escalation pages (`p3968r0` p1, `p4012r0` p3) also unit-failed with `content_omission`. Six pages escalation-sanctioned while unit returned review/fail (`p3428r3`, `p3962r0`, `p3978r0` ×3, `p4231r0`). Impact: dropping escalation in favor of unit-only on scheduled overlap pages **does not regress** the 2 confirmed misses; it may **tighten** review on 6 pages (verdict-drift risk toward more review, not fewer findings).

- [MED] Proposed unification (minimal diff): **Defer `_escalate_page` when `page:{N}` is already in `selected_unit_ids` from `run_unit_checks`**, then **synthesize** `page_escalations[]` post-unit from `content_omission` defects with `candidate_status=candidate_not_found` (`pdf_judge.py:811-818` shape). Keep a fallback `_escalate_page` when the screen flagged a page that quota skipped (possible when `MAX_UNIT_CHECKS=5` and >5 risky units; not observed in fleet: 0 screen-flagged-not-escalated papers). Preserve separate sidecar keys for schema v6 consumers (`fusion.py`, `inspect_report.py`). Impact: **18 calls removed**, ~**22.5 s** fleet wall; evidence class change from page-attributed `content_missing` bool to defect-group `content_omission` with `affected_count` — fusion must read synthesized entries, not live `PageJudgment` output.

- [MED] Non-overlapping unit checks dominate PDF LLM volume and are not substitutable for escalation. Fleet: **782** PDF `page:*` unit checks vs **18** page escalations; **764** unit-only pages triggered by other router signals (`table_presence`, `heading_drift`, `token_delta`, `missing_captions` at `source_router.py:184-263`). Overlap signal mix on the 18 duplicate pages: **18× low_recall**, plus **13× heading_drift**, **13× table_presence**. Impact: unification addresses only the low-recall double-call; table/heading routing stays unit-only.

- [LOW] Serial ordering guarantees escalation evidence enters `evidence_verification` before unit evidence today (`pdf_judge.py:791-791` then `904-926`), but both fold to the same `review` cap independently (`pdf_judge.py:825-827`, `927-932`). Dedupe does not change worst-axis verdict logic if synthesis preserves confirmed-missing quotes in `grounded_quotes`.

## False-pass hypothesis

Skip escalation and synthesize `content_missing=false` from a unit **pass** on a screen-flagged page where the model would have returned `content_missing=true` with verbatim quotes under `PAGE_JUDGE_SYSTEM_PROMPT`. Measured overlap: **0/2** confirmed escalation pages unit-passed (both unit-failed). Residual risk: **10/18** overlap pages unit-passed after escalation ran (sanctioned anyway); a future rerun could flip under MoE variance. Mitigation: synthesize `content_missing` only from grounded `content_omission` + `candidate_not_found`, not from unit `verdict=pass` alone; cap at review when screen recall stays below floor regardless of unit pass.

## False-fail hypothesis

Prompt-fusing `PAGE_JUDGE_SYSTEM_PROMPT` and `UNIT_CHECK_SYSTEM_PROMPT` into one call causes the model to over-report `content_omission` / table defects on pages where the narrow escalation prompt correctly sanctioned TOC/front-matter under `CONVERSION_CONTRACT`. Multi-rubric batching drops long-context judge accuracy by double digits (RuVerBench, `05-web.md` Q5). Prefer skip-and-synthesize over prompt merge.

## What would change my mind

A re-run of the 9-paper dev-replay set (`corpus/dev-replay/labels.json`) with skip-and-synthesize showing **zero** verdict regressions (pass→fail or fail→pass) vs baseline, and at least one holdout paper where quota skips a screen-flagged page proving the fallback `_escalate_page` path still fires.
