# 109 - sglang-MoE-Support

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions — sglang's DeepSeek-V4-Pro stack is production-grade (registry, dsv4 kernels, DPA+DeepEP recipes), but our alliance-pod profile (single 8×H200, FP8 Pro, vLLM `--max-num-seqs 16`) has no verified sglang cell that proves higher *effective* concurrency; migration is a full engine swap plus 381-paper A/B, not a slot-count upgrade.
**Confidence:** medium

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] **DeepSeek-V4-Pro is first-class in the model registry.** Architectures `DeepseekV4ForCausalLM`, `DeepseekV4ForCausalLMNextN`, `DeepseekV4ForCausalLMDSpark` are recognized (`python/sglang/srt/configs/model_config.py:123-128,679-684,839-841`); loader entry `EntryClass = [DeepseekV4ForCausalLM]` (`python/sglang/srt/models/deepseek_v4.py:3088`). Family overrides pin `--attention-backend dsv4`, `page_size=256`, `swa_full_tokens_ratio=0.1`, and NVFP4 MoE runner auto-select (`python/sglang/srt/arg_groups/overrides.py:784-824`). KV cache defaults to FP8 e4m3 on CUDA (`overrides.py:1463-1484`). Default `max_running_requests=256` when unset (`python/sglang/srt/arg_groups/deepseek_v4_hook.py:49-53`). Impact: engine swap is technically feasible on alliance-pod hardware class, but `max_running_requests` is a scheduler cap, not proof of MoE-safe decode width. Quality risk: none from registry alone; runtime numerics differ from vLLM.

- [CRITICAL] **Our pod shape mismatches sglang's verified H200 Pro FP8 recipe.** Cookbook lists `sgl-project/DeepSeek-V4-Pro-FP8` for Hopper (`docs_new/src/snippets/configs/deepseek-ai/deepseek-v4.jsx:50-52`) but every verified H200 Pro FP8 cell is **multi-2** with `--tp 16 --dp 16 --enable-dp-attention --moe-a2a-backend deepep --max-running-requests 32` (`deepseek-v4.jsx:1148-1215`); benchmark slots for those cells are **empty** (`deepseek-v4-benchmarks.jsx:356-363`). Single-node 8×H200 Pro verified cells exist only for **FP4** (`deepseek-v4.jsx:1267-1314`). alliance-pod is 8×H200 single-node on vLLM (`SERVICES.toml:64-68`, `00-baseline.md:65-66`). Impact: no in-clone evidence that sglang on *our* topology beats vLLM 16 slots; closest single-node Pro data is FP4, not our FP8 checkpoint path. Quality risk: deploying an unverified flag combo without A/B is high.

- [HIGH] **Attention is dsv4 compressed-attention (FlashMLA tiers), not classic V3 MLA — but DPA is the documented DeepSeek throughput lever.** V4 uses `DeepseekV4AttnBackend` with FlashMLA sched metadata for C4/C128 compression pools (`python/sglang/srt/layers/attention/deepseek_v4_backend.py:75,191-199`; registry `attention_registry.py:156-161`). Classic MLA dispatch (`MLA`, `trtllm_mla`) applies to DeepSeek V3/R1 (`overrides.py:404-444`; unit test `test/registered/unit/models/test_deepseek_mla_dispatch.py:39-55`). DPA is explicitly recommended for DeepSeek MoE production: `--dp-size 8 --enable-dp-attention` + `--ep 8 --moe-a2a-backend deepep` (`docs_new/docs/advanced_features/dp_dpa_smg_guide.mdx:489-504`). V4 MoE forward wires the no-EP DPA gather/scatter path when `attn_dp_size > 1` and `moe_a2a_backend` is `none` (`deepseek_v4.py:1693-1760,2197,2270`). Impact: RadixAttention + DPA could cut per-call prefill cost on our shared ~3.7k system prefix (`00-baseline.md:48-52`, `05-web.md:74-80`) even at the same running-request count, but DPA is **not auto-on** for V4 (`server_args.py:1067-1074` defaults `False`; overrides only set it for CP/DSA branches). Quality risk: DPA trades low-concurrency latency for high-concurrency throughput (cookbook GLM-5 notes in `dp_dpa_smg_guide.mdx` ecosystem).

- [HIGH] **Expert parallelism is mature (DeepEP, EPLB, waterfill) but obeys the same MoE batch-width physics as vLLM.** EP via `--moe-a2a-backend deepep` with `ep_size=tp_size` is wired into MLA-CP and DSA-CP overrides (`overrides.py:355-356,425-426`). DeepEP dispatch buffer constraint documented: `max-running-requests × MTP_draft_tokens ≤ SGLANG_DEEPEP_NUM_MAX_DISPATCH_TOKENS_PER_RANK` (`DeepSeek-V4.mdx:175-177`). sglang's own H200 Pro FP4 single-node benchmark annotates **KV capacity ~15 concurrent requests** — tok/s/GPU pinned from conc 64 upward; excess concurrency queues (`deepseek-v4-benchmarks.jsx:408-411`). vLLM persona measured 16→32 slots +57% wall from MoE expert-union growth (`98-vllm-moe-serving.md:9-12`). Impact: sglang `max-running-requests 256` does not override KV/MoE physics; on 8×H200 Pro we should expect ~15-32 effective decodes, not 256. Raising running requests without co-tuning DeepEP env is a throughput *loss* at best. Quality risk: MoE batch-composition variance at wider batches (same concern as vLLM `VLLM_BATCH_INVARIANT`).

- [MED] **No DeepSeek-specific vLLM vs sglang throughput comparison exists in the clone.** Closest vLLM benchmarks are Llama-3.1-8B/70B (`benchmark/benchmark_vllm_060/README.md:3-14`, dated v0.3.0 vs v0.6.0). sglang DeepSeek-V4 benchmarks are self-referential only (`deepseek-v4-benchmarks.jsx:1-68`, v0.5.15 on B200/B300). Web cards cite generic SGLang +29% baseline / up to 6.4× prefix-heavy vs vLLM (`05-web.md:79-80`) but not DeepSeek-V4-Pro at concurrency 16 on H200. At conc 16, sglang H200 Pro FP4 single-node reports `tpot_ms: 23.12`, `tokens_per_sec_per_gpu: 559` for isl=8192/osl=1024 (`deepseek-v4-benchmarks.jsx:403-405`) — not directly comparable to our ~70 tok/s per-request judge decode (`00-baseline.md:26-28`) without identical prompt shapes. Impact: cannot claim sglang beats vLLM 16 slots from clone evidence alone; any win requires measured A/B on alliance-pod with our judge payloads. Quality risk: none from reading docs; high from assuming blog ratios transfer.

- [MED] **Operational cost of switching: redeploy + client backend + full verdict revalidation.** alliance-pod and h200x8-deepseek-v4-pro both declare `backend = "vllm_thinking"` (`SERVICES.toml:48,65`); pipeline AgentBackend paths assume vLLM thinking-block semantics. sglang exposes OpenAI-compatible `/v1` (cookbook curl, `deepseek-v4.jsx:69-71`) but structured-output / thinking-parser behavior differs (sglang ships `--reasoning-parser deepseek-v4`, `--tool-call-parser deepseekv4` in test recipes — `test/registered/amd/test_deepseek_v4_pro_fp8.py:84-87`). Switching engine changes token distributions → **quality-stability violation without 381-paper fingerprint A/B** (`00-baseline.md:77-78`). Pod is uptime-billed (`SERVICES.toml:62-63`), so migration downtime has fixed cost regardless of call volume. Impact: even a 20% server-side win is swamped if revalidation blocks the sprint; engine swap is a parallel track, not a drop-in slot bump. Quality risk: **primary** — silent verdict drift across engines.

- [LOW] **Prefix-heavy judge workload is where sglang's RadixAttention could help without raising slots.** Shared `CONVERSION_CONTRACT` (~3.7k chars) prefixes most calls (`00-baseline.md:48-52`); guard tag suffix breaks only the tail. sglang RadixAttention reuses leading prefixes tree-wide (`05-web.md:78-79`); vLLM APC on DSv4 needed retention fix for sliding-window layers (`05-web.md:5-12`). If alliance-pod stays on vLLM, PR #43447-style retention may be the lower-risk prefix win; if switching to sglang, RadixAttention is native but still needs guard-tag placement experiment. Impact: per-call latency reduction at fixed 16 effective slots could contribute to 5-10 min target (other personas' APC lever), orthogonal to concurrency ceiling. Quality risk: low if outputs unchanged; prefix-cache pollution under mixed loads (`05-web.md:84-85`).

## False-pass hypothesis

Migrating to sglang with `--max-running-requests 256` (V4 default, `deepseek_v4_hook.py:49-53`) or cookbook high-throughput cells without KV-aware tuning runs 64+ client papers against a ~15-slot MoE+KV ceiling (`deepseek-v4-benchmarks.jsx:408-411`); queueing inflates latency uniformly while MoE batch mix shifts numerics, so borderline unit checks flip pass/review without any markdown change — same silent drift class as vLLM 16→32 (`98-vllm-moe-serving.md:24-26`).

## False-fail hypothesis

Enabling sglang DPA (`--enable-dp-attention --dp 8`) on a single-node 8×H200 Pro deployment without the verified multi-node FP8 recipe changes attention/MoE grouping vs vLLM TP+EP baseline; a paper that passed on alliance-pod vLLM could fail review on sglang due to engine numerics alone, even with identical candidate markdown — the mandatory A/B would surface this as apparent "stricter judging."

## What would change my mind

A bench on alliance-pod hardware (8×H200, FP8 Pro, client c=32) comparing vLLM `--max-num-seqs 16` vs sglang with tuned DPA+DeepEP (`--dp 8 --enable-dp-attention --moe-a2a-backend deepep`, DeepEP env co-tuned per `DeepSeek-V4.mdx:175-177`) on **identical judge prompts** (monolith + unit-check shapes from `00-baseline.md:43-45`), showing ≥15% wall reduction on a 381-paper subset **and** verdict fingerprints match the vLLM baseline — or sglang-published H200 Pro FP8 single-node benchmarks filling the empty cells at `deepseek-v4-benchmarks.jsx:356-363`.
