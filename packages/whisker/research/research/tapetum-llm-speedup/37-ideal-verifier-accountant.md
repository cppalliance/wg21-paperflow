## False-pass hypothesis

Running ideal verify **first** and short-circuiting the source-aware cascade when ideal returns `verdict="agree"`: a candidate can match the human ideal on structure while still missing PDF-sourced content (token-preserving semantic corruption the monolith catches). The source-aware lane would never run, fusion would see no `missing_content` / unit defects, and advisory coverage would false-clear on ideal-agree alone despite independent judge mandate (`whisker/CLAUDE.md` "source remains the highest authority").

## False-fail hypothesis

Persisting the completed judge sidecar on `IdealVerificationError` while leaving `status="error"` (today fail-not-partial discards the full chain): fusion already treats `status="error"` as unusable (`fusion.py:154-159`), so operators see **error** despite a logged `verdict=review` with 5 unit checks (P4182R0). Ideal-only retry fixes infrastructure waste but leaves **error** semantics unless fusion learns partial-success tombstones.

## What would change my mind

Sidecar inventory showing **>3** fleet papers with ideals (ideal dir grew without this persona's glob), or **>2** `IdealVerificationError` among the **6** v10 tombstones, would raise ideal-attributable error share above **33%** and make selective retry a first-class warm-run lever; per-call ideal latency histogram with p99 **>120 s** would justify a dedicated per-call cap over the 900 s paper budget.
