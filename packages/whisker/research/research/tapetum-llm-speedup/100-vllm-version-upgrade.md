# 100 - vLLM-Version-Upgrade

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions — upgrading alliance-pod from v0.24.0 to v0.25.1 is low-risk and delivers incremental DSv4/MLA/scheduler kernel gains (~90–240 s on the 3003 s cold run), but the big wins (APC retention, MTP, MBT 16384) require server flags on builds already present in v0.23+; version bump alone cannot reach 5–10 min.
**Confidence:** high (release notes + clone anchors; pod semver confirmed v0.24.0 in prior persona probe)
