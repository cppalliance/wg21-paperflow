# 10 - olmocr-pipeline-coverage

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions — Copy the enumerate-every-page orchestration, retry budget, and aggregate sidecar counts; do not copy pdftotext fallback-as-success or aggregate-only fallback accounting for our fail-closed LLM verification mode.
**Confidence:** high

## Findings
- [CRITICAL] Full-page extraction coverage is architectural, not routed: `process_single_pdf` reads `num_pages` from `PdfReader` and spawns one `process_page` task per page via `asyncio.TaskGroup` for `range(1, num_pages + 1)`. Evidence: `olmocr/pipeline.py:532-551`. Impact: every physical page enters the VLM path; this is the clearest prior art for our planned `--all-pages` loop over `page_count`, unlike our current PDF lane where only 5 of 15 pages get scoped LLM checks (00-baseline.md:19-22).
- [HIGH] Permanent VLM failure does not fail the page; it substitutes pdftotext. After `max_page_retries` (default 8), `process_page` returns `make_fallback_result`, which calls `get_anchor_text(..., pdf_engine="pdftotext")` and sets `is_fallback=True` while keeping `is_valid=True`. Evidence: `olmocr/pipeline.py:233-250`, `371-375`, `1221`; `olmocr/prompts/anchor.py:52-60`. Impact: olmocr guarantees every page has *some* text, not that every page was VLM-processed; copying this wholesale would let our golden-review mode false-pass pages the LLM never judged (00-baseline.md:7 distinguishes extraction from verification coverage).
- [HIGH] Document-level fail-closed exists only for excessive fallback rate, not per-page VLM success. If `num_fallback_pages / num_pages > max_page_error_rate` (default `0.004`, ~1/250), the whole document is discarded (`return None`); otherwise fallback pages proceed with a warning. Evidence: `olmocr/pipeline.py:557-567`, `1222`. Impact: on a 15-page golden PDF, one pdftotext fallback still ships; there is no assert that `vlm_pages == page_count`. Our planned fail-closed check against `page_count` must be stricter than olmocr's ratio gate.
- [MED] Per-page coverage accounting for callers is aggregate-only. Dolma metadata exposes `pdf-total-pages` and `total-fallback-pages` plus per-page span arrays, but no per-page `is_fallback` or `vlm_succeeded` list in `attributes`. Evidence: `olmocr/pipeline.py:624-649`. Impact: a downstream auditor can see *how many* pages missed VLM, not *which*; our `PdfJudgeResult` sidecar should emit an explicit per-page bitmap (planned in 00-baseline.md:44).
- [MED] Retry and backoff before fallback are substantial: temperature escalation (`TEMPERATURE_BY_ATTEMPT`), rotation-aware sequential retries, optional parallel retries when the vLLM queue is empty, and up to 10 connection backoffs per attempt (`try_single_page_with_backoff`). Evidence: `olmocr/pipeline.py:85`, `281-375`, `253-278`. Impact: retry discipline is worth copying for review-mode serial unit calls; note connection exhaustion calls `sys.exit(1)` and kills the entire job, not one paper gracefully (277-278).
- [MED] Work-queue batching is PDF-centric, not page-skipping. `WorkQueue.populate_queue` groups whole PDF paths by estimated `pages_per_group`; intra-PDF concurrency is the TaskGroup above, not queue omission. Evidence: `olmocr/work_queue.py:106-111`, `olmocr/pipeline.py:1377-1408`. Impact: olmocr's "no skipped pages" guarantee lives inside `process_single_pdf`, matching how we should wire coverage inside `judge_pdf_extraction` rather than in CLI routing alone.
- [LOW] Runtime telemetry (`completed_pages`, `failed_pages`, `finished_on_attempt_*`) and `WorkerTracker` page states (`started`/`finished`/`errored`) are log/metrics only, not returned to the caller as an auditable artifact. Evidence: `olmocr/pipeline.py:1492-1510`, `olmocr/metrics.py:7-101`. Impact: ops visibility exists; structured audit fields for golden review must be added by us (cf. our defect: `--inspect` dead on PDFs, 00-baseline.md:20-21).
- [LOW] olmocr-bench post-conversion coverage is deterministic per-page unit tests, not pipeline runtime LLM verification. Bench requires an MD output per PDF page (`benchmark.py:69-72`, `94-99`); every page carries a mandatory `BaselineTest` (non-empty, no runaway repetition). Evidence: `olmocr/bench/tests.py:484-545`; https://olmocr.allenai.org/papers/olmocr.pdf (05-web.md Q3). Impact: useful prior art for *golden QA after conversion*, orthogonal to tapetum's in-flight LLM judge; aligns with 00-baseline.md:31 that no surveyed repo runs LLM-per-page verification.

## False-pass hypothesis
On a 500-page batch PDF, page 17's VLM call fails all 8 retries; olmocr emits pdftotext text with `is_fallback=True`, `total-fallback-pages=1`, and still builds the Dolma document because `1/500 = 0.2% < max_page_error_rate 0.004`. An operator counting "every page has output" would falsely believe full VLM coverage.

## False-fail hypothesis
Two VLM failures on a 15-page golden PDF (`2/15 ≈ 13.3% > max_page_error_rate 0.004`) causes `process_single_pdf` to return `None` and drop the entire document even though 13 pages are good VLM output — over-penalizing at document scope if we copied the ratio threshold into review mode without scaling to small-n papers.

## What would change my mind
Evidence in the clone that dolma output (or markdown export) includes a per-page `is_fallback`/`vlm_processed` array and the pipeline fails closed when any page is fallback on single-document invocations — would upgrade to **usable** as a direct verification-coverage template.
