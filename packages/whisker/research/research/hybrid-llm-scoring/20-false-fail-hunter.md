# 20 - False-fail hunter

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions — a merged det+LLM verdict is viable ONLY with an asymmetric, gated-upgrade fusion; every symmetric family (worst-of, flag-additive, agreement-escalation) and the fail-locked matrix from persona 13 inherits the deterministic lane's 61% review inflation and destroys the one thing the 2026-07-06 run proved tapetum does: clear 101 of 188 adjudicated reviews at confidence >= 0.95.
**Confidence:** high

## Findings

- [CRITICAL] **Worst-of fusion zeroes tapetum's entire proven rescue value: 101 det-review papers with llm=pass would ALL stay merged=review, and 3 det-pass papers would be dragged DOWN.** Evidence: full parse of the 204 `data/whisker/*.whisker.tapetum.json` sidecars on disk: cross-tab whisker x tapetum = review/pass **101**, review/review 77, review/fail 10, fail/review 9, pass/pass 4, pass/review **3** (P3724R3, P3724R4, P4211R0, all cosmetic heading minors at conf 0.95-0.98). Sample cleared sidecar: `n5034.whisker.tapetum.json` (whisker review on `unigram drift 0.132 > 0.1`, unigram 0.9528, ref_nid 0.9227; tapetum pass conf 0.98, "None. The conversion is faithful and fully readable."). Deterministic lane: 232 review / 15 fail / 134 pass of 381 (`00-baseline.md:47`). Under `worst-of(det, llm)` the merged review count is 232 + 3 = **235 (62%)**, strictly worse than either lane alone. Failing rule: worst-of on raw verdicts (persona 13's candidate (c), already flagged at `13-score-fusion-architect.md:23`). Surviving rule: asymmetric upgrade — `det=review(soft-only) + llm=pass(conf >= floor, grounded) -> merged=pass`. Impact: goal 3's merged verdict is worthless for triage if it cannot clear what the LLM already cleared; worst-of makes the merged lane a strict superset of the det queue.

- [CRITICAL] **The fail-locked matrix recommended by persona 13 re-fails all 9 heading-monotone RESCUE papers the LLM explicitly adjudicated as cosmetic.** Evidence: fail/review pairs on disk: N5035, N5037, N5043, P3161R5, P3647R1, P3941R2, P3941R3, P3941R4, P4160R0 — every one a `gate:heading_monotone` sole hard flag (e.g. `n5035.whisker.json` hard=`['gate:heading_monotone:heading level jumps H1 -> H3']`, unigram 0.9526) with tapetum review conf 0.95-0.98 and a single `structure/review/minor` finding ("Cosmetic heading-level jump (H1 -> H3) that does not impair readability"). Persona 13's rule "never to fail unless whisker is already fail... combined fail iff whisker already fail" (`13-score-fusion-architect.md:29`, decision row `whisker=fail -> combined=fail` at `:51-56`) locks merged=fail for all 9; its own false-fail hypothesis admits this (`13-score-fusion-architect.md:67`). The RESCUE population exists precisely because these are false det fails (`adjudicate.py:94-96`, `_is_heading_only_fail` at `adjudicate.py:128-134`; RESCUE prompt case at `tapetum_llm.md:66-68`). Failing rule: unconditional whisker-fail lock. Surviving rule: one carve-out — `det=fail(heading_monotone-only) + llm in {pass, review} -> merged=review` (never pass: C1-respecting demotion of a hard gate needs a human). Impact: 9 of 15 det fails (60%) are legitimately shrinkable to review; a merge without the carve-out has ZERO rescue power on the fail bucket.

- [HIGH] **Chunk aggregation carries two false-fail amplifiers into any merge that consumes the folded LLM verdict: min-confidence poisoning and a severity-fold bypass.** Evidence: (1) `aggregate_adjudications` takes `confidence = min(a.confidence for a in parts)` (`chunking.py:259`) and the decide step demotes ANY non-pass below `CONFIDENCE_DECISION_FLOOR` to review (`adjudicate.py:284-285`) — one shaky chunk of a 5-chunk paper caps the whole paper. (2) `_worst_part_verdict` (`chunking.py:311-316`) promotes any part-level `fail` to overall `fail` with NO severity fold when `axis_findings` is empty — the only aggregation path that bypasses the `fail+minor -> review` rule of `worst_axis_verdict` (`chunking.py:106-123`). This is the chunk worst-axis folding gap already filed as `llm-stack/11-false-positive-hunter.md` finding 6 ("one cosmetic structure hit anywhere in a multi-chunk paper becomes overall review"). Runtime: 4 chunked sidecars on disk; N5044 and P3045R7 are tv=review from exactly one `structure/review/minor` finding, P3045R8 and P4215R0 are clean passes. Impact: for oversize papers, a merged verdict inherits a worst-axis fold across chunks that is one empty-findings hallucination away from a hard merged fail; merge must consume `axis_findings` + severity, not the folded verdict string.

- [HIGH] **67 of 89 tapetum reviews (75%) are structure-only cosmetic (exactly one non-pass axis: `structure/review/minor`), so any agreement-weighted or additive family cements noise instead of clearing it.** Evidence: reproduced parse (67/89 tv=review sidecars); matches the 84/106 ratio of the prior run in `llm-stack/11-false-positive-hunter.md` finding 1. On these papers the two lanes flag DIFFERENT surface defects for the same shippable conversion: e.g. N5045 det soft=`['unigram drift 0.124 > 0.1']` vs llm `structure/minor` ("body starts at H3 instead of H2"); P3596R0 det soft=`['3 uncertain marker(s)', 'qa_score 65 < 70']` vs llm structure-minor. A rule like "both lanes non-pass -> merged=review-confirmed (higher priority)" or "sum of flags across lanes" reads two cosmetic observations as corroborated defect. Failing rule: agreement escalation / flag-count addition. Surviving rule: fold `structure+minor+sole-non-pass-axis` to pass-equivalent BEFORE fusing (the decide-step fold `llm-stack/11` finding 1 asks for), then fuse verdicts. Impact: without the pre-fusion fold the merged lane keeps ~60 of the 77 review/review papers in the queue on cosmetics alone.

- [HIGH] **Errored LLM lane must merge as whisker-only, not unknown: 4 of the 6 papers that errored in the 2026-07-06 run (P2728R11, P2728R12, P3568R2, P3904R1 per `00-baseline.md:51`) now hold llm=pass conf 0.98 sidecars after rerun; blocking them at merged=unknown would have withheld 4 clearable papers.** Evidence: on-disk `p2728r11/p2728r12.whisker.tapetum.json` tv=pass conf 0.98 (whisker review on `ref_nid 0.017` advisory only — a broken oracle, not a broken conversion), `p3568r2` tv=pass conf 0.98 (det review on 135 misaligned regions + drift 0.371, unigram 0.9987), `p3904r1` tv=pass conf 0.98; the remaining two (p3951r1, p4233r0) rerun to tv=review. Additional trap: the pipeline-failure stub returns `suggested_verdict=review, confidence=0.0` (`adjudicate.py:490-501`) — a naive merge cannot distinguish that synthetic review from a real LLM review. Failing rule: `llm absent/errored -> merged=unknown` (blocks) or naive consumption of the 0.0-confidence stub. Surviving rule: `tapetum_available=false -> merged = whisker verdict, combined_rule="whisker_only"` (persona 13 finding at `13-score-fusion-architect.md:12`), treating `confidence == 0.0` as unavailable. Impact: C5 fidelity is satisfied by DISCLOSING the missing lane in the merged artifact, not by degrading the verdict.

- [MED] **Double-counting one defect across lanes pushes additive merges a tier below either lane: the heading jump on N5035 surfaces three times.** Evidence: `n5035.whisker.json` carries hard=`gate:heading_monotone` AND correlated soft flags `9 misaligned region(s)` + `reference text agreement 0.662` (the heading shift misaligns regions and depresses the weak oracle's nid; sibling N5034 without the gate has ref_nid 0.9227), while `n5035.whisker.tapetum.json` files the SAME defect as `structure/review/minor`. Any score-subtractive or flag-counting fusion tallies >= 3 penalties for one cosmetic H1->H3 jump; det alone says fail, llm alone says review, an additive merge says fail-with-margin. Failing rule: per-flag penalty accumulation across lanes. Surviving rule: verdict-level fusion only, with defect-family dedupe (heading gate <-> structure axis; lossy_table_count <-> tables axis) if any evidence joining is displayed. Impact: goal 3's "combined score" must not be arithmetic over the union of both lanes' flags.

- [MED] **44 of the 232 det reviews were never adjudicated at all (benign-region-only exclusion), so the merged lane silently equals det=review for them; a merge that does not label this state overstates LLM coverage.** Evidence: 177 whisker sidecars have no tapetum sidecar; 44 of these are verdict=review, and every single one is misaligned-region-only (e.g. P0876R22 `['2 misaligned region(s)']`, P3098R2 `['35 misaligned region(s)']`) — exactly the `_is_benign_region_only` skip (`adjudicate.py:116-125`, floor `REGION_BENIGN_UNIGRAM_FLOOR`). These are the papers candidate selection itself deems lowest-risk, yet they stay in the human queue forever because neither lane can clear them. Impact: merged reporting needs three coverage states (llm-cleared / llm-confirmed / llm-never-looked, cf. C5); and the cheapest legitimate review-shrink of all may be a det-side benign-region fold, no LLM required.

## False-pass hypothesis

P2728R11: whisker review rests solely on `reference text agreement 0.017 low (advisory)` while unigram coverage is 0.9985; tapetum passes at conf 0.98. If the near-zero oracle agreement were ever a real symptom (e.g. the reference converter read a different document revision and the tomd output silently mixed pages), the gated-upgrade rule I recommend (`det=review(soft-only) + llm=pass -> merged=pass`) would clear a corrupted paper, because ref_nid is advisory and the LLM reads only the converted markdown, never the PDF. Mitigation: exclude `ref_nid < 0.10` (pathological oracle disagreement) from the auto-upgrade set, or require the upgrade to log the overridden soft flags in the merged artifact.

## What would change my mind

A blind human-labeled holdout over the 101 review/pass sidecars showing that fewer than ~85% are truly shippable (i.e. the LLM's conf-0.95+ passes on det-reviews are materially unreliable). That would invalidate the quantified claim that a gated asymmetric upgrade legitimately clears 101 of 232 reviews now (44%), rising toward ~160-200 of 232 with the structure-minor pre-fusion fold (~60 more of the 77 review/review papers) and a det-side benign-region fold (44 more) — and would push me toward persona 13's no-upgrade matrix despite its total loss of rescue value.
