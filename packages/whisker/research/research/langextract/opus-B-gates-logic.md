## The single most load-bearing logic finding (with file:line)

**Wrong-span acceptance in Tier-3 LCS fuzzy is not a hypothesized edge case: it is codified as expected behavior in langextract's own regression suite.** `tests/fuzzy_alignment_cases_test.py:213-223` (`gapped_lcs`) asserts that a 3-token extraction grounds to a **9-token** char span containing six intervening unrelated tokens, and the gate that lets it through sits exactly at its floor: 3 matches / 9-token span = density `0.333 == _FUZZY_ALIGNMENT_MIN_DENSITY` (`resolver.py:1403-1404`, constant `resolver.py:58`). This is the single gate that decides whether a returned `char_interval` is trustworthy provenance, and the target ships a test that pins the untrustworthy answer as correct. Everything downstream that treats `char_interval` as evidence location inherits this.

## Net assessment (3-6 sentences)

The persona claims about langextract's own logic hold up under direct verification: the three-tier aligner (monotonic exact DP -> difflib lesser -> LCS fuzzy), the 0.75/0.333 gates, the drop-or-raise resolver with no schema-retry, and the 10-wide shipped concurrency defaults are all exactly where the personas said they were. The architecture is genuinely stronger than tapetum's boolean-drop grounding (real char spans, graded `AlignmentStatus`, occurrence disambiguation via the Pareto-frontier DP), and the index-keyed ordered-yield pattern is sound and matches our `gather_concurrent` shape. But the load-bearing weakness is confirmed and self-inflicted: the fuzzy gate accepts gapped composite spans at its density floor, and the maintainers' own oracle test blesses that behavior, so `char_interval` provenance from Tier 3 cannot be trusted without a post-alignment substring check. Combined with the confirmed fail-open defaults (`suppress_parse_errors=True`, no schema-noncompliance retry, ungrounded extractions retained with `char_interval=None` and no partial flag), the safe adoption boundary is: take Tier-1 monotonic exact DP + status/span return + plural stemming, keep tapetum's document-level `partial_ratio >= 0.90` (or a span re-check) instead of importing Tier-3 LCS, and never inherit the concurrency or suppress-error defaults.
