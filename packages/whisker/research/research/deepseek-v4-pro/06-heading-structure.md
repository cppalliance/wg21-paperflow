# 06 - Heading-Structure

**Verdict:** usable-with-conditions — strong general long-document comprehension, but no published eval on ATX heading-hierarchy audit; tapetum's structure axis depends on prompt obedience and grounded evidence, both weakened by V4-Pro's hallucination and long-context gaps.
**Confidence:** medium

## Findings

- [HIGH] **No benchmark directly tests the tapetum `structure` task (ATX level audit + section-order fidelity).** DeepSeek-V4-Pro's published long-context scores (LongBench-V2 51.5 EM, CorpusQA 1M 62.0 ACC, MRCR 1M 83.5 MMR) measure retrieval and QA over long inputs, not whether a model can judge markdown heading trees or detect permuted WG21 sections. READOC evaluates heading detection via TEDS for PDF-to-Markdown *extraction* systems, not LLM adjudicators; V4-Pro is absent from that benchmark family. Evidence: `00-baseline.md` benchmark table; arXiv:2606.19348 Table 7/8; READOC ACL 2025 Findings (heading TEDS for DSE pipelines). Impact: external numbers are only weak proxies; adoptability of V4-Pro for the structure axis is unproven until a WG21-labeled smoke set exists.

- [HIGH] **Long-context retrieval degrades before our largest unchunked papers, risking missed section-order defects.** V4-Pro MRCR 8-needle stays above 0.82 through 256K tokens but falls to ~0.59 at 1M (HuggingFace V4 blog, Figure 9; `00-baseline.md` §5.2). tapetum splits at `MAX_PAPER_MD_CHARS = 500_000` (`00-baseline.md` §3), keeping most calls under the degradation cliff, but within-chunk section-order checks still require scanning hundreds of H2 sections linearly. CorpusQA 1M (62.0 vs Opus 71.7) confirms V4-Pro trails the frontier on realistic multi-document QA (`00-baseline.md` §1). Impact: permuted or duplicated sections mid-document are the dominant structure defect tapetum must catch (`tapetum_llm.md` line 52); compressed attention may miss non-local ordering errors that a human reader would spot.

- [HIGH] **94% hallucination / near-zero abstention makes invented structure evidence likely.** `00-baseline.md` §5.1 cites Artificial Analysis AA-Omniscience: V4-Pro hallucinates on 94% of queries where it should abstain. The structure axis requires verbatim `evidence_spans` for dropped/reordered sections; `ground_spans` drops ungrounded quotes, but a confident hallucinated section-drop still wastes the deep-model call and can demote to `review` via sub-floor confidence. Impact: structure findings are the hardest to ground (must quote heading lines or section-lead prose); high invention rate is a direct threat to trustworthy structure adjudication.

- [MED] **Pre-training explicitly targets long scientific documents, which partially covers WG21 heading patterns.** arXiv:2606.19348 §4.1 states V4 pre-training "place[s] a particular emphasis on long-document data curation, prioritizing scientific papers, technical reports, and other materials that reflect unique academic values" over 33T tokens. LongBench-V2 (+11.3pp vs V3.2-Base to 51.5) includes single-document QA over academic papers (Bai et al., ACL 2025). Impact: ATX heading syntax (`##`–`######`) and deep numbered hierarchies are in-distribution at a coarse level; the model likely *reads* heading levels correctly even though nobody measures monotonicity audit accuracy.

- [MED] **Live agent runs show DeepSeek models conflate numbered ATX headings with list hierarchy.** DeepSeek-Reasonix commit 6da057d documents a production failure: the model wrote plan phases as `### 1. Phase` with indented bullets; a parser treating only list items as hierarchy collapsed everything to level 1. The fix explicitly steered the model toward plain numbered lists instead of numbered headings. Impact: analogous risk for tapetum: a model may misread WG21's mix of ATX depth, numbered clause titles, and list-nested wording as flat or wrongly nested structure, especially under thinking-mode token pressure.

- [MED] **tapetum rubric and whisker gate disagree on heading monotonicity; V4-Pro must obey the rubric, not mirror the gate.** whisker's `_gate_heading_monotone` hard-fails H2→H4 jumps (`gates.py:96-112`); 9/14 reference-free hard fails are heading-only with uni≥0.997 (`00-EVIDENCE-BASELINE.md` §3c). tapetum explicitly calls this cosmetic: "A heading-level jump ... is cosmetic: severity `minor`, verdict `review` at most, never `fail`" and defines a RESCUE population for heading_monotone-only fails (`tapetum_llm.md` lines 52, 64-66). Impact: V4-Pro's value on the structure axis is rescuing false-fails, not replicating whisker pedantry; failure to follow the rubric would invert the lane's purpose for 64% of current hard fails.

- [LOW] **tomd design choices are documented in-prompt but require negative knowledge.** The conversion contract states body starts at H2, front matter is YAML (implicit H1), and known unnumbered sections are H2 (`tapetum_llm.md` lines 35-36). Chunked input rules forbid flagging missing sections at chunk boundaries (lines 91-93). Impact: a model trained on generic markdown may flag stripped front matter or H2-first body as corruption unless it internalizes tomd's contract; the prompt mitigates this but is not constrained-decoded (`00-baseline.md` §4, schema-in-prompt path).

- [LOW] **whisker already computes deterministic heading trees via mistune (same engine as tomd QA).** `metrics.py:653-693` builds an APTED-scored heading tree from ATX headings with front matter stripped. tapetum does not expose this tree to the LLM; structure adjudication is pure model judgment plus evidence grounding. Impact: an opportunity exists to feed a heading outline as trusted metadata (out of scope here), but today V4-Pro must re-derive hierarchy from raw markdown inside the untrusted envelope.

## False-pass hypothesis

A WG21 paper whose major H2 sections are permuted (e.g., "Wording" appears before "Motivation" and "Design" is duplicated under a near-identical heading) but whose ATX levels are monotone and unigram coverage is high. whisker's order-blind content gate and heading_monotone gate both pass; tapetum triage at fast tier might also pass if the model scans locally but never compares global section sequence. V4-Pro deep adjudication could accept the conversion because every section *exists* and heading levels look well-formed, missing the order defect tapetum's rubric reserves `fail` for.

## False-fail hypothesis

P3941R2-class paper: sole whisker hard flag is `heading_monotone: H2 -> H4` with uni=0.999 (`00-EVIDENCE-BASELINE.md` §3c). Despite the RESCUE rubric, V4-Pro flags `structure` at severity `major` or overall `fail`, citing the level jump as structural corruption. Decide folds non-major fails to `review`, but a `major` mislabel still surfaces as a false structural fail and contradicts the lane's rescue mandate.

## What would change my mind

A labeled tapetum smoke set on ≥20 WG21 papers with human structure-axis labels showing: (1) ≥90% recall on injected section permutations/drops, and (2) ≥90% correct `review`/pass (not `fail`) on the heading_monotone-only rescue set — would flip the verdict to **usable**.
