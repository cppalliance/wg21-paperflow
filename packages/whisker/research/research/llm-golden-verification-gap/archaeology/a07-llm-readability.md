- [CRITICAL] **PR #286 table-cell wrap (SF→S) was predicted as a table-semantics / token-preserving false-pass class, not as a tapetum routing fix.** Persona 18 named first-match cell binding, HTML-table blindness, and neighbor-only checks that miss column swaps (`research/llm-readability/18-table-semantics-auditor.md` findings #1,#3,#4; `facts.py:374-423`). Impact: vertical wrap in poll-table header cells is a subtype of "table corruption invisible to neighbor facts and absent from LLM lane signals"; research named the class, implementation never added per-cell compare or table-page routing priority. Answer-class: **2** (no signal type) + **1** (wrap reading is a golden-contract rule, not encoded in prompts).
