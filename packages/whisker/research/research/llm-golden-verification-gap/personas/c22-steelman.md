## False-pass hypothesis

An operator reads PR #286 fused `review` + conf 0.98 alongside metadata/outline `pass` and zero accepted defect groups, interprets `review` as "LLM verified issues exist," and merges — falsely believing the poll-table blocker was machine-detected when fusion only asserted incomplete unit coverage (`00-baseline.md:52-53`, `PR286-LLM-FALSE-CLEAR.md:435`). This is a *reporting* false-pass, not a fused `pass`; det-only would have been the actual false-clear.

## False-fail hypothesis

A clean golden with complete source-aware coverage, metadata `pass`, llm `pass`, and no unchecked units could still yield fused `pass` via `llm_clear_soft_review` when det is soft-review-only (`fusion.py:458-476`) even if table cells were never compared — structurally possible, not observed on PR #286/PR #295; would be a false-fail relative to "human must review everything" workflow, not relative to advisory design.

## What would change my mind

A replay of PR #286/PR #295 where fusion returns merged `pass` (agree with det) while human-verified blockers remain, OR a dated decision memo overriding `llm-qa-integration/SYNTHESIS.md` with holdout proof that source-aware LLM uniquely catches >15% of det+facts misses at unchanged false-pass rate (`SYNTHESIS.md:150-152`) — neither exists; until then, the lane's job on these incidents was triage demotion, and it performed that job.
